Help with Cora Mobile, your hardware and your account

Back to home

Questions about Cora Mobile, your Cora hardware, your subscription or your account? Email us and we'll get back to you.

Contact Us

  • Email: cora@coraiq.tech
  • Response time: we aim to reply within 1–2 business days
When you write, please include the email address on your Cora account, your device model (iPhone, iPad or Android) and a short description of the problem. It helps us answer faster.

Getting Started

  • Download Cora Mobile, create an account, and add your first tank.
  • Log water parameters manually, or connect a controller or Cora device and the readings come in on their own.
  • Open Reef Buddy for your daily check-up, and ask Cora Assistant about your tank.

Pairing a Cora Device

  • Power on your Cora device and keep your phone nearby.
  • In Cora Mobile, open the Devices tab and tap Add Device.
  • Follow the steps on screen to connect the device to your Wi-Fi.
  • When it's paired, the device shows up on your dashboard and starts sending readings.

If a device won't pair, check that your phone's Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are on, the device has power and is close by, and your Wi-Fi is a 2.4 GHz network. Still stuck? Email us the device model and what you see on screen.

Subscriptions & Purchases

  • Plans: Cora has Plus, Pro and Max subscriptions, and you can start with a free trial. The free tier is always there.
  • Restore purchases: open Settings → Account & Subscription and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or cancel (iOS): in the App Store, go to your Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Manage or cancel (Android): in Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions.
  • Billing questions: email us and we'll help sort it out.

The Cora Assistant & Your Data

Cora Assistant uses AI to answer your questions. When you use it, your questions (or voice recordings) and your tank context (parameters, livestock, notes, and connected-device information) are sent to a third-party AI service that generates the response. Cora Mobile asks for your permission before it does this the first time. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what is collected and how it is used.

Account & Privacy

  • Delete your account: you can remove your account and all its data inside Cora Mobile. See Delete Your Account.
  • Privacy: read our Privacy Policy for what we collect and how it's used.
  • Terms: read our Terms & Conditions.
