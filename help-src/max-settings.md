Open settings with the **gear** at the far right of the top bar.

![Cora Max settings](img/max-settings.webp "Cora, then your tanks, then Cora Max Settings.")

Settings has three groups. **CORA** holds things that apply to all the Cora devices in your household. **TANKS** has one row per tank. And there's a single **Cora Max Settings** row for everything about this screen alone.

:::note Two kinds of setting
Some settings belong to your **account**. They're the same whichever phone or Cora Max you look at, and changing one changes it everywhere. Others belong to **this screen** only, like its brightness or Wi-Fi. The CORA and TANKS groups are account settings. Cora Max Settings, the third group, holds this screen's own settings.
:::

## CORA

These apply to every Cora device on your account.

| Row | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Automations** | Every scene for all your tanks, with a filter by tank. More in [Scenes on Cora Max](/help/max-automation) |
| **Devices** | The equipment Cora controls, for all your tanks. More in [Devices and device health](/help/max-devices) |
| **Cora Assistant** | Which device answers when someone says "Hey Cora." More in [Talking to Cora](/help/max-voice) |

## TANKS

Each tank has a row that opens its own settings.

- **Alert thresholds** sets the healthy range for each metric. More in [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).
- **Units** picks the units this tank uses for salinity, temperature, alkalinity, volume, phosphate and nitrate. With **Auto**, each reading shows in the unit its source recorded it in. If you pick a specific unit, everything on this tank is converted to it, on every screen and when the Assistant speaks.
- **Primary Cora Max** is the device that reads this tank's equipment. More in [Devices and device health](/help/max-devices).
- **Dashboard layout** is this tank's Cora Max layout. More in [Editing the Cora Max dashboard](/help/max-dashboard-editing).
- This tank's records and reports are here too: **Journal**, **Maintenance**, **Livestock**, **Reef Buddy**, **Health Reports** and **ICP Reports**.

## Cora Max Settings: everything about this screen

The last row in Settings carries this Cora Max's name. It opens everything that applies to **this screen only**, in these groups.

| Group | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Display** | Brightness, dim-after timer and its brightness, night dimming, showing the clock, and waking the screen on an alarm |
| **Sound & Voice** | Audio output (internal speaker, 3.5 mm or Bluetooth), the alert chime and its volume, spoken alerts, and **Wake-word listening** on or off |
| **Reef Buddy** | The hour the daily briefing appears, and whether it's read aloud |
| **Child Lock** | On or off, and how long before it locks. How to unlock it is in [Talking to Cora](/help/max-voice) |
| **Notifications** | Notification history, the inbox for your whole account with every briefing, alarm and account notice |
| **Language** | The one language your whole account uses. See the note below |
| **Network & Updates** | Wi-Fi, firmware updates, and a view-only line showing how often this Cora Max reads your devices (set from Cora Mobile) |
| **Status** | One card for each tank this screen shows, with polling status, last poll and last cloud write. View only |
| **Help & About** | Help & Support, and About (version and **Licenses**) |
| **Reset** | Re-pair this device, and factory reset |

:::note Language applies to your whole account
When you change **Language**, every phone and every Cora Max on your account switches with it. New reports, alerts and briefings come in the new language. Reports and alerts you already have stay in the language they were written in and aren't translated again.
:::

![Notification history on Cora Max](img/max-notifications.webp "Alarms and their recoveries, newest first, across every tank.")

Recoveries are logged as well as alarms. So when a parameter goes out of range and comes back, you see both halves instead of a warning with no follow-up.

## Pairing, re-pairing and factory reset

**Cora Max Settings → Reset** shows what this screen is paired to right now, and gives you two options.

**Re-pair this device** clears this screen's pairing and takes it back to the pairing screen.

**Factory reset** also clears every preference saved on this screen. Its confirmation tells you exactly what's cleared and what's kept.

Neither one touches the tank data already saved to Cora Cloud. Both remove this Cora Max from your account and make it forget its Wi-Fi network, so you pair it again from Cora Mobile like a new one. If Cora Max can't reach Cora Cloud to remove itself, it tells you it's still listed in Cora Mobile. Open it there under **Devices** and choose **Remove device**.

![The factory reset confirmation](img/max-factory-reset.webp "The dialog shows what's cleared and what's kept before you go ahead.")

:::warning A reset clears the screen, but your data stays
Factory reset removes the pairing and every setting stored on the device, and takes it back to its pairing screen. **Tank data already saved to your account is kept.** Pair the device to a tank again and it comes back. What you lose is this screen's own setup, like its Wi-Fi, display, sound and voice settings.
:::

:::note You may get your layouts back
Before a reset, Cora Max saves a copy of its configuration. If you pair it back to the **same tank**, it offers **Restore previous layout for this tank?** Take it, or choose **Start fresh** if you wanted a clean screen.
:::

## Which tanks this screen shows

A Cora Max can show one tank or several, and you switch between them from the top bar. You choose which tanks it shows **on your phone**, under **Devices → your Cora Max**.

## Updates

Cora Max keeps itself up to date. New versions download in the background and install themselves, and Cora Max tells you what changed. If a version has just arrived, you'll see a prompt on screen. You don't need to do anything to stay current. More in [Keeping Cora Max updated](/help/max-updates).
