Activity is the log of every attempt to change something on your equipment, and what happened to it. It's the same log your phone shows. Look here first when something you asked for doesn't seem to have happened.

Open it from the **tank menu** (tap the tank name in the top bar), or go to **Settings → [your tank] → Activity**.

![The activity log on Cora Max](img/max-activity.webp "The legend at the top explains each outcome.")

## What each entry tells you

Each row shows the equipment and what was asked, for example *Return: Turned off*, and the time. It also tells you two more things.

**What asked for it.** For example **Tapped on this Cora**, **Voice on this Cora**, **Cora app**, **Voice in the Cora app**, **Automation rule** or **Sent from the cloud**.

**How it travelled.** **LAN** means it went over your own network. **VIA CLOUD** means it went through Cora Cloud. If you have more than one Cora Max, the entry also says which one carried it out.

## The four outcomes

The legend at the top of the screen repeats these.

| Outcome | Means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | The equipment reported back that it did it |
| **Unconfirmed** | Sent, but nothing reported back. We don't know whether it worked |
| **Refused** | Didn't run. A safety rule, a lock or the equipment itself turned it down |
| **No change** | It was already in the state you asked for |

Under a **Refused** or **Unconfirmed** entry, a line says why, and what to check before you try again. For example: *No device answered in time, so it MAY still have run. Check the equipment before repeating this.*

## When something didn't happen

On a touchscreen, a command that was sent but never acknowledged looks the same as one that worked. This screen shows you the difference.

1. Find the entry for what you asked.
2. If it's **Refused**, read the reason under it. Nothing ran.
3. If it's **Unconfirmed**, check the equipment itself before you try again, so you don't do it twice.

If it still doesn't add up, see [Establishing why something changed](/help/troubleshooting#establishing-why-something-changed).

## Reading it alongside the journal

Activity shows what the system did. The [journal](/help/max-records#journal) shows what you did by hand. Between them, they explain most surprises.
