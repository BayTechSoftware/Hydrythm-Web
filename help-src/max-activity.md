Cora Max shows the same activity log as your phone. It lists every **actuation request** (every attempt to change something) and what happened to it. A refused or unconfirmed request may never have reached the equipment at all, and that's exactly why it's logged.

Open it from the **tank menu** (tap the tank name in the top bar), or go to **Settings → Tank settings → [your tank] → Activity**.

![The activity log on Cora Max](img/max-activity.webp "The legend stays at the top of the screen, so you never have to remember what an outcome means.")

## What each entry tells you

Each row shows the action, the equipment and the time. Two more details are the ones to look at.

**What asked for it.** You at this screen, you on your phone, voice, the Assistant, an [automation](/help/mobile-automation) rule, a smart button, or your account. If you did it at the wall, the entry says so.

**How it travelled.** Whether the command went over your own network or through your account, and which Cora carried it out. If you have more than one Cora, this tells you which one acted.

## The four outcomes

| Outcome | Means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | The equipment reported back that it did it |
| **Unconfirmed** | Sent, but nothing reported back. We don't know whether it worked |
| **Refused** | Didn't run. A safety rule, a lock or the equipment itself turned it down |
| **No change** | It was already in the state you asked for |

## Why the outcome matters at the wall

When you tap, a command that was sent but never acknowledged looks the same as one that worked. This screen shows you the difference.

If something you asked for doesn't seem to have happened, check here before you decide the equipment has failed. Usually the request was refused, or nothing ever reported back.

## Reading it alongside the journal

Activity shows what the system did. The [journal](/help/mobile-journal) shows what you did by hand. Between them, they explain most surprises.
