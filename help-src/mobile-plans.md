Monitoring your tank is free, with no limit. The paid tiers give you bigger AI allowances and add Reef Buddy.

## What is always free

- Live readings from your equipment, with dashboards and charts
- Devices, alerts and thresholds
- Journal, maintenance and livestock
- Push notifications for critical alerts
- **One tank**

## The free allowances

| Feature | Free |
|---|---|
| Assistant messages | 20 a month. Voice uses the same pool and costs more per turn |
| Health reports | 1 a month |
| ICP analysis | **One, once**. You get a single analysis for the life of the account, then it's locked |
| AI on tanks | Your primary tank only |
| Reef Buddy | Not included |

When an allowance runs out, you can still read everything Cora has already made for you.

## The trial

Your **30-day Pro trial starts when you sign up**. It doesn't wait until you first use a feature. It includes Reef Buddy and the higher allowances.

When it ends, your account goes back to Free. **Nothing is deleted.** You can still read every reading, journal entry, report and briefing you have.

## Paid tiers

There are three tiers, **Plus**, **Pro** and **Max**, each monthly or annual. You'll find current prices in Cora Mobile and in your app store, since that's where you pay.

| | Plus | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assistant messages / month | 500 | 1,200 | 2,500 (fair use) |
| Voice sessions / month | 100 | 200 | 400 |
| Health Reports / month | 4 | 12 | 100 |
| ICP analyses / month | 1 | 3 | 30 |
| Tanks with AI monitoring | 1 | 5 | 20 |

Voice sessions have their own count, and every spoken reply also uses up your message allowance. Every paid tier includes Reef Buddy, ICP upload and analysis for the labs Cora reads, and comparison with your own history.

Go to **Settings → your account** to open **Account & Subscription**.

![Account and subscription](img/mobile-plans.webp "Your plan and its renewal date, with the store controls below it.")

It shows your current plan and when it renews. Below that are three controls.

| Control | Does |
|---|---|
| **Redeem code** | Applies a promotional or partner code |
| **Manage Subscription** | Opens your store's subscription settings |
| **Restore Purchases** | Brings back a purchase made on another device, or before you reinstalled |

## Seeing what you have used

![Usage and limits](img/mobile-usage.webp "Each metered feature against its monthly allowance.")

**Settings → Cora Assistant → Usage & Limits** shows each metered feature next to its monthly allowance, all on one screen. That's Assistant messages, ICP analyses and health reports.

You can also see usage where you use each feature.

- In **Assistant**, this month's messages are shown above the message box.
- In **Intelligence**, this month's ICP analyses and health reports are shown under the buttons.

Check these when you want to know how much you have left this month.

## Changing plan

Use **Manage Subscription**. Changes go through the App Store or Google Play and follow the same rules as any other subscription on your phone, including that store's refund and cancellation terms.

## Cancelling

You cancel in the App Store or Google Play. You can't cancel inside Cora. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then goes back to Free.

:::note Cancelling doesn't delete anything
Your tanks, readings, history and journal stay as they are. You lose the paid features, but you keep your data. Come back later and it's all still there.
:::

## If your plan is not showing

Sometimes a purchase takes a moment to reach Cora Mobile. If it's been a few minutes, try these.

1. Close Cora completely and open it again.
2. Make sure you're signed in to the same account that made the purchase.
3. Make sure the store account on your phone is the one you bought with.

If it still isn't there, tap **Restore Purchases**. If that doesn't work, email **[cora@coraiq.tech](mailto:cora@coraiq.tech)** with the date you bought it.
