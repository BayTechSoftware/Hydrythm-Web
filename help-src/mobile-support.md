To report a problem or send feedback, use **Settings → Help & Support → Report a Bug / Send Feedback**. Reporting from inside Cora Mobile attaches details that are hard to remember later.

![The Send feedback form in Cora Mobile](img/mobile-support.webp "Pick what kind of thing it is, say what happened, and add a screenshot if it helps.")

## What you fill in

1. Under **What kind of thing is it?**, pick one: **Something is broken**, **Feature request**, **Wrong data**, **Confusing**, **Billing** or **Something else**. That gets the report to the right person.
2. Under **What happened?**, write it in your own words.
3. Tap **Send**. You'll see *Thanks, we got your report.*

Say what you expected and what you saw. "Alkalinity showed 2 d old on the dashboard when the Apex was reading fine" helps far more than "readings broken".

You can send up to 10 reports a day.

## What is attached automatically

Every report includes a short line of context. The form shows it to you before you send, under *Sent with your report*.

- the **Cora Mobile version**
- your **platform** and OS version
- the **screen** you were on

The report also carries your phone's language and the tank you had selected. With that, we can usually reproduce what went wrong.

## Screenshots

**Attach a screenshot** adds an image from your photos to the report. **Remove screenshot** takes it off again before you send. Take the screenshot first, then open the form.

:::warning Cora support will see your screenshot
Cora Mobile tells you this above the button. Check the image before you attach it. A dashboard screenshot shows your tank names and readings, plus anything else that was on screen.
:::

:::note No screenshots in sitter mode
When you're looking at someone else's tank through a shared link, you can't attach a screenshot. It would put *their* data in *your* support ticket, and they'd never know.
:::

## Automatic crash reports

**Send crash reports**, in **Help & Support** under the report row, does the same job without you having to notice something broke and write it up. If Cora Mobile stops or a screen fails to load, it sends the technical details. No tank data, photos or account details are included. See [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings#getting-help).

## Emailing support

**Settings → Help & Support → Contact Support** opens an email to **[cora@coraiq.tech](mailto:cora@coraiq.tech)** with your Cora Mobile version filled in. We usually reply within a day. If your phone has no mail app, write to that address from any email account.
