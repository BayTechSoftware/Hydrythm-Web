The Reef Room is the overview of every tank this Cora Max shows. Open it with the **grid icon** at the far left of the top bar. On screen, it's called **Cora Tanks**.

![The Reef Room](img/max-reef-room.webp "Each tank with its health score, headline parameters and anything out of range.")

## The layout {#the-layout}

A Cora Max shows **up to four tanks**, and the Reef Room gives each one an equal share of the screen.

| Tanks | Layout |
|---|---|
| 1 | One large tile |
| 2 | Side by side |
| 3 | Three across |
| 4 | Two by two |

With one tank, Cora Max opens on that tank's dashboard. The grid icon still takes you to the Reef Room.

:::note Four tanks is the limit
You can assign at most four tanks to a Cora Max, when you pair it or later on your phone under **Devices → your Cora Max**. Once a Cora Max has four tanks, the **Add Tank** button is gone on your phone, so remove one first. If you have more tanks than that, use a second Cora Max. Each one keeps its own set. The steps are in [Setting up Cora Max](/help/max-setup#changing-which-tanks-it-shows).
:::

## What a tile shows {#what-a-tile-shows}

Each tile sums up one tank.

- A coloured bar down the left edge, for the worst state on that tank.
- A **health score** in a ring (with one to three tanks). Next to it is a line like *3 of 37 parameters out of range* (or *All 37 parameters in range*), and how many are near their limits.
- A status chip: **N out of range**, **N to watch** for readings near their limits, **Healthy**, or **Monitoring** while there's nothing to grade yet.
- The tank's key readings. Wider tiles add a 24-hour trend line to each one.
- **When it last updated**, and **Open dashboard**.

The health score looks at how far out each parameter is and how much it matters, so lots of good readings can't hide one serious problem. It only reads 100% when nothing is out of range. If a major parameter like alkalinity, calcium or magnesium is far out of range, the score can't go above 33%. A heavy metal far out of range can bring it down to 0%. Parameters near their limits are counted on the tile but don't lower the score.

Under the tiles, a strip shows today's insights and each tank's health headline in turn. Tap a card in the strip to open that tank's [Reef Buddy](/help/max-intelligence).

## Moving between tanks {#moving-between-tanks}

- **Tap a tile**, or **Open dashboard**, to go to that tank.
- **Swipe sideways** on the dashboard to move between tanks and back to the Reef Room.
- **The grid icon** takes you back to the Reef Room from any tank page. You won't see it on the Reef Room itself.

Each tank has its own dashboard layout, so the screen changes completely as you move between them. More in [Editing the Cora Max dashboard](/help/max-dashboard-editing).
