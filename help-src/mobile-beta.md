Some of Cora's newest integrations are in Beta. We're still testing them, so some readings or controls may be limited and what you see can change between updates.

:::note Beta
**Beta.** The Beta integrations are only open to Beta users. If you are not a Beta user you will not see them in **Add device**. To ask for Beta access, contact [support](/support.html).
:::

## Who gets Beta

Beta access belongs to your Cora account. It's switched on by us, for an account, and you can't turn it on yourself in Settings. Being in a store testing programme doesn't switch it on either. To ask, write to [support](/support.html) from the email on your account.

Once your account is a Beta user, the Beta rows show up in **Add device** on Cora Mobile. You don't need to restart the app.

If you're not a Beta user, **Add device** lists Cora, Neptune Apex, Red Sea, Jecod and AquaWiz. Equipment you've already linked stays on your Devices tab.

## The Beta integrations

Each one is added from **Devices → Add Device**. A row marked **Requires Cora Max** is greyed out until the tank has a Cora Max, because that Cora Max does the reading.

| Integration | In a line | Needs a Cora Max |
|---|---|---|
| Maxspect | Gyre pumps: wave pattern and speed. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections#maxspect). | Yes |
| GHL | ProfiLux and Mitras controllers: probes, sockets, dosers and level sensors. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections#ghl-profilux-and-mitras). | Yes |
| HYDROS | Readings and, with a write key, control, through the HYDROS cloud. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections#hydros). | No |
| Focustronic | Alkatronic, Dosetronic and Mastertronic, through your Focustronic account. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections#focustronic). | No |
| EcoTech | Radion lights, VorTech pumps and Versa dosers. See [EcoTech lights, pumps and dosers](/help/mobile-ecotech). | Yes |
| Inkbird | IAM-T1 and IAM-T2 room air monitors. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections#inkbird-room-air-monitor). | Yes |
| Camera | Wyze, Tapo, Sonoff and other RTSP cameras, plus Skimmer Watch. See [Cameras and Skimmer Watch](/help/mobile-cameras). | Yes |

## Labelled Beta, open to everyone

A few features carry **(Beta)** in their name but don't need Beta access. Everyone sees them.

- **Large doses (Beta)** on a DŌS head, described in [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control)
- **Dosing plan (Beta)** on a ReefDose
- **Pump schedule (Beta)** on a ReefRun
- **ReefMat settings (Beta)** and **New roll (Beta)** on a ReefMat

## What Beta means day to day

- A Beta page may not match this guide the week after an update. If something doesn't work as described, tell us from [Getting help](/help/mobile-support).
- Some commands go through the maker's own servers or app, which Cora doesn't control. The page says so where it matters.
- Equipment we haven't been able to test on real hardware is marked as such.

Cora Max shows the Beta devices your account has linked. See [Devices and device health](/help/max-devices).
