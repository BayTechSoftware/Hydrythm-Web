Cora Max shows the same records as your phone, but you can't change all of them here. This is what you can do at the wall.

| Area | On Cora Max |
|---|---|
| **Log parameters** | Type in test results |
| **Journal** | Read entries and add new ones. Log **Fed extra** or **Skipped a feed** in one tap |
| **Maintenance** | Mark a task **Done** or **Snooze** it. You create and edit tasks on your phone |
| **Livestock** | View only. Add and edit on your phone |

All of them are in the **tank menu** (tap the tank name in the top bar) and under **Settings → [your tank]**. Anything you add here shows up on your phone right away, and the other way round.

## Logging readings

Open **Log parameters**, tap a parameter, type the reading with the on-screen keypad and save. It goes straight to the tank's dashboard and history, so you can record a test at the tank where you did it. You can also say *"Hey Cora, log alkalinity 8.1."*

Cora checks each number before it saves. If one looks like a typo, or is far outside your last 30 days, it asks **Double-check these values?** and may suggest what you meant (*"did you mean 8.1?"*). Tap **Go back** to fix it, or **Save anyway** if the reading is right.

If a newer reading for that parameter is already showing, your value says **Saved to history only**. It's kept in the history and charts, and the dashboard keeps showing the newer one.

## Journal

Open **Journal** from the tank menu and tap **New entry**. Give it a title, and add a description, tags or a photo if you like. **Attach water params** saves the current readings with it. Or say *"Hey Cora, log that I fed mysis."* More about this in [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).

### Feeding

At the top of the journal screen, **Fed extra** and **Skipped a feed** each add one journal entry. A line below them shows your feeding routine, for example *Feeding routine: Flakes: 2 pinches, twice a day*. You set the routine in Cora Mobile. With none saved, the line says *Set your feeding routine in the phone app*.

If Cora Max reads your Neptune Apex, it also logs a Feed A to D cycle when one starts, whether you started it here, at the Apex or in Apex Fusion.

:::note There's no journal icon in the top bar
The icon next to the devices icon is **Reef Buddy**, and its dot means today's briefing is unread. You add journal entries from the tank menu or by voice.
:::

## Maintenance

![Maintenance on Cora Max](img/max-records.webp "Tasks due, with Snooze and Done on each, and what was completed recently.")

The top of the screen says how many tasks are due. Below it, **Upcoming** lists each task with **TODAY**, **IN 3d** or **OVERDUE 2d**, and **Completed · last 7 days** shows what you've done lately.

- Tap **Done** when you finish a task. If it's overdue, Cora asks **When did you do it?**
- Tap **Done earlier…** if you did it before today. Pick the date and time, and the next due date counts from then. A time in the future, or before the last completion, isn't accepted.
- Tap **Snooze** to push it back by **1 Day**, **3 Days**, **1 Week**, **2 Weeks** or **1 Month**.

You add and change tasks on your phone. More about this in [Maintenance](/help/mobile-maintenance).

## Livestock

This is the full inventory, with what's **In the tank** and what's **No longer in the tank**. It's view only here. You add and edit livestock in Cora Mobile, and what you add there shows up on this screen right away. More about this in [Livestock](/help/mobile-livestock).

:::note One set of records
Each tank has one journal, one livestock inventory and one maintenance list. Cora Max and your phone show the same records.
:::
