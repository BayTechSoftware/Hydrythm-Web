Cora Max shows the same records as your phone, but you can't edit all of them here. What you can do at the wall depends on the area.

| Area | On Cora Max |
|---|---|
| **Journal** | Read and add entries |
| **Readings** | Log test results with the on-screen keyboard |
| **Maintenance** | Complete or snooze a task. You create and edit tasks on your phone |
| **Livestock** | View only. Add and edit on your phone |

Anything you add here shows up on your phone right away, and the other way round.

You'll find all of them in the **tank menu** (tap the tank name in the top bar).

## Journal

You can add an entry without leaving the tank. You're usually standing at the wall screen when you do the work anyway.

Open the **tank menu** (tap the tank name in the top bar) and tap **+** to add an entry. Or say *"Hey Cora, log that I fed mysis."* More about this in [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).

:::note There's no journal icon in the top bar
The icon next to the devices icon is **Reef Buddy**, and its dot means today's briefing is unread. You add journal entries from the tank menu or by voice.
:::

## Livestock

This is the full inventory, grouped by type, with the same statuses as on your phone. It's **view only** here. You add and edit livestock in Cora Mobile, and what you add there shows up on this screen right away. More about this in [Livestock](/help/mobile-livestock).

## Maintenance

This is the task list with its compliance figure and overdue count. You can mark a task done as soon as you finish it. More about this in [Maintenance](/help/mobile-maintenance).

## Logging readings

Cora Max has an on-screen keyboard for typing in test results, so you can record a test at the tank where you did it.

:::note One set of records
Each tank has one journal, one livestock inventory and one maintenance list. Cora Max and your phone show the same records.
:::
