You can talk to Cora Max. It's the easiest way to use it when your hands are wet or you're across the room. Voice on Cora Max is Cora Assistant, the same assistant you use on your phone.

## Before you start

- **AI data sharing has to be on.** Turn on **Allow Cora Assistant to use saved tank data** in Cora Mobile, under **Settings → Cora Assistant**. While it's off, Cora Max shows **Voice needs your OK** and nothing you say is sent. More in [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant#consent).
- **Voice uses your monthly AI allowance.** Voice sessions have their own monthly count, and each spoken reply also uses messages from the same Assistant allowance as your phone, more than a typed one. Your plan sets both. More in [Plans and subscriptions](/help/mobile-plans).

## Two ways to start

Say **"Hey Cora"**. Cora Max listens in the background for the wake phrase and starts a session when it hears it.

Or tap the **Cora Assistant** icon in the top bar. Cora starts listening right away and answers out loud. There's no confirmation step, so if you tapped it by mistake, tap **Cancel**. Nothing is lost.

## Sound and wake word settings

![Alert sound, Spoken alerts and Wake word switches in Cora Max Settings, with the Reef Buddy briefing switch below](img/max-voice.webp "Alert sound, Spoken alerts and Wake word, with Reef Buddy below them.")

These are in **Settings → Cora Max Settings → SOUND & VOICE**, and apply to this screen only.

- **Wake word** turns background listening on or off. With it off, tapping the **Cora Assistant** icon still works, so use this if you'd rather the screen wasn't listening all the time.
- **Speaker** picks where sound comes out: **Internal**, **3.5 mm** or **BT** (Bluetooth). For voice, the internal speaker is the weakest of the three. **BT** opens **Pair a Bluetooth speaker**, where you put your speaker in pairing mode and pick it from the list.

## Which device answers "Hey Cora"

If you have more than one Cora Max at home, only one of them answers the wake phrase. That's the **Answering device**. It's a separate setting from the device that reads your equipment, which is the **Primary Cora Max** (more in [Devices and device health](/help/max-devices)).

Change it in **Settings → Cora Assistant** on any Cora Max, or in Cora Mobile. It applies to your whole household.

Only the **Answering device** listens for "Hey Cora". Your other Cora Max screens don't listen while it's online. If it goes offline, or hasn't been seen for more than 15 minutes, another Cora Max starts listening in its place, and stops again once it's back. The switch takes about a minute, so if nobody answers "Hey Cora" right after one, say it again or tap the **Cora Assistant** icon.

## What you can ask

Questions about the tank:

- *"What's my alkalinity?"*
- *"Has the temperature been stable today?"*
- *"When did I last change water?"*
- *"Why is pH lower than usual?"*
- *"Compare the last six months of my magnesium with my latest ICP."*

You don't need to repeat yourself for follow-ups. After a question about the display tank, *"and the frag tank?"* works.

## What Cora can do by voice

- Switch a Neptune Apex outlet on, off or back to auto: *"Turn the skimmer off."* *"Put the fan back on auto."*
- Start an Apex feed cycle (Feed A to D): *"Start feed mode."*
- Start a Trident test: *"Start an alkalinity test."*
- Change an alert range: *"Set the pH alert to 7.9 to 8.4."*
- Log a test result: *"Log alkalinity 8.1."*
- Write in the journal: *"Log that I did a twenty litre water change."* *"Log that I fed extra mysis."* *"Log that I skipped the morning feed."*
- Control Red Sea equipment: dose from a ReefDose head, change its daily dose, start a ReefATO+ fill, advance a ReefMat, or set a ReefRun pump's speed.
- Control Jecod pumps.

## What Cora can't do by voice on Cora Max

- Run or stop a scene. Use the scene tile or **Settings → Automations**.
- Dose from a Neptune DŌS head. Use the device's page on Cora Max.
- Change Cora Max's own settings, add devices or edit the dashboard.

Cora Assistant on your phone can do some of these. More in [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant#what-cora-can-change).


## Confirmations

Before anything reaches your equipment, Cora checks with you. It tells you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your OK. You can answer out loud, in your account's language.

If what you said could mean more than one thing (you have two heaters and said "the heater"), Cora asks which one instead of guessing.

Anything Cora does to equipment shows on screen with its result, and is logged in [Activity](/help/max-activity).

:::warning Confirming is your call
The confirmation is there so nothing catches you by surprise. Read or listen to it before you agree, especially for anything that switches off life support or doses.
:::

## What Cora can see

For the tank on screen: your live readings and how old each one is, up to about six months of history for any parameter, your recent journal and your lab results.

If a reading is stale, Cora tells you so and doesn't treat it as current.

## Which tank Cora is talking about

It's whichever tank the screen is showing. Switch tanks in the top bar first, or name the tank in your question.

## Stopping

Say *"stop"* or *"goodbye"*. You can also tap **Cancel** while Cora is listening, or **Stop talking** while it's speaking. If nobody speaks for about 30 seconds, the session ends by itself. Cora stops listening when the session ends.

## Child lock and voice

While [Child lock](/help/max-settings#child-lock) has locked the screen, Cora still answers questions, but it refuses anything that changes your equipment.

## If it mishears

Reef words are hard, and equipment names are harder. If Cora keeps mishearing a device, give it a more distinct name. Go to **Settings → Devices**, tap the pencil next to the device (or **Rename** on its page), and type a short, everyday name. The new name shows on your phone too.

## If Cora doesn't respond

- Check that **Wake word** is on (**Settings → Cora Max Settings → SOUND & VOICE**), that this Cora Max is your **Answering device** (see above), and that its volume is up.
- A noisy sump or a filter right next to Cora Max can stop it hearing the wake phrase. Move it, or speak a bit closer.

If Cora Max shows a message instead of listening:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Voice needs your OK** | Turn on AI data sharing in Cora Mobile (**Settings → Cora Assistant**), then try again |
| **Free voice limit reached** | This month's allowance is used up. Upgrade your plan in Cora Mobile, or wait for next month |
| **Too many requests** | Wait a moment, then try again |
| **Connection issue** | Check this Cora Max's Wi-Fi, then tap the **Cora Assistant** icon again |

If that doesn't help, have a look at [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
