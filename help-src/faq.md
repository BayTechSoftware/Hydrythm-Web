## Getting started

### Do I need a Cora Max, or is my phone enough? {#do-i-need-a-cora-max}

You don't need to buy anything to start. Cora Mobile on its own tracks your tanks, logs tests, sends alerts and runs Cora Assistant.

A Cora Max at the tank adds a few things. Without one, your phone reads a Neptune Apex, Red Sea or Jecod device only while Cora Mobile is open on the same Wi-Fi. A Cora Max reads them around the clock, keeps your readings current while you're away, and passes on commands you send from away. More in [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

### How do I add my first tank? {#how-do-i-add-my-first-tank}

After you sign up, Cora Mobile asks three questions: a name, the type of tank and how much water. Only the name is required. More in [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup).

### Can I try Cora before I sign up? {#can-i-try-cora-before-i-sign-up}

Yes. On the sign-in screen, tap **Explore a demo reef**. It's a sample tank, and you don't need an account. More in [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup).

### My tank is still cycling. Can Cora help? {#my-tank-is-still-cycling}

Yes. Tap **Starting a new tank?** on the dashboard to start the cycling journey. The card only shows on a tank with no readings yet, so do it before you log a test or link equipment. More in [Guided journeys](/help/mobile-journeys).

### What does "tank profile" mean, and why does it ask so many questions? {#what-does-tank-profile-mean}

The tank profile describes your system: size, livestock, lighting, and how you dose and test. Cora uses your answers to set sensible starting ranges, and to make Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant talk about your tank instead of reefs in general. You can skip any section. More in [Your tank profile](/help/mobile-tank-profile).

### How long does setup take? {#how-long-does-setup-take}

One tank with no equipment takes a few minutes. Each device needs its own pairing step, so that part takes longer. You can add more later.

## Cora Max

### Can one Cora Max show more than one tank? {#can-one-cora-max-show-more-than-one-tank}

Yes, up to four. [The Reef Room](/help/max-reef-room) shows them side by side. For more tanks, add a second Cora Max.

### Can two Cora Max screens show the same tank? {#can-two-cora-max-screens-show-the-same-tank}

Yes. Add both to the same tank and they show the same live data. **Primary Cora Max** only decides which one talks to the tank's equipment. More in [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device).

### What is "Primary Cora Max", and do I need to set it? {#what-is-primary-cora-max}

It decides which Cora Max reads a tank's equipment. Most people never touch it. With one Cora Max there's nothing to choose. The default, **Any active (automatic)**, lets every online Cora Max share the job. Pick a specific one only if you want a single device doing it. More in [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device#primary-cora-max).

### Which device answers "Hey Cora" if I have more than one Cora Max? {#which-device-answers-hey-cora}

Only one Cora device answers at a time. You choose it in Cora Mobile under **Settings → Cora Assistant**. It has nothing to do with Primary Cora Max. More in [Talking to Cora](/help/max-voice).

### Why can't Cora Mobile find my Cora Max when I try to pair it? {#why-cant-cora-mobile-find-my-cora-max}

A new Cora Max is found over Bluetooth. Turn Bluetooth on, allow Cora to use it, and stand near the screen. You also need at least one tank in Cora Mobile. After the Wi-Fi step, your phone talks to Cora Max over Wi-Fi, so stay on the same network until pairing finishes. Each error message and its fix is in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#pairing-a-cora-max-fails).

### Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi as my Cora Max? {#does-my-phone-need-the-same-wi-fi}

Only while you pair it. After that, you can use Cora from anywhere. A few of a device's own settings still need your phone on that device's network.

### Does Cora Max need the internet, or just my home Wi-Fi? {#does-cora-max-need-the-internet}

Both. It needs your home Wi-Fi to reach your equipment, and the internet to reach Cora Cloud for syncing, your other devices and the Assistant. The status tag in the top bar shows which one it has. More in [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour).

### What happens if my internet goes down? {#what-happens-if-my-internet-goes-down}

Cora Max and Cora Mobile keep showing the last data they have and keep working with equipment on their own network. Syncing between devices, voice and the Assistant come back when the connection does.

### Can I move Cora Max to a new Wi-Fi network or router? {#can-i-move-cora-max-to-a-new-wi-fi}

Yes. On Cora Max, join the new network under **Settings → Cora Max Settings → Wi-Fi**. Your tanks, devices and account stay the same. Equipment on the old network goes out of reach until it joins the new one, so reconnect each device the way you first added it. More in [Setting up Cora Max](/help/max-setup).

## Cora Mobile

### Why does Cora Mobile ask for Local Network and Bluetooth access? {#why-local-network-and-bluetooth}

It needs Local Network access to find Wi-Fi equipment and reach a Cora Max on your home network, and Bluetooth to pair a Cora Max and equipment that uses Bluetooth. Without them, it can't find the device. More in [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings).

### Can I check on my tank when I'm not home? {#can-i-check-on-my-tank-away}

Yes. With a Cora Max at the tank, your readings stay current wherever you are. Without one, a Neptune Apex, Red Sea or Jecod device shows the last readings your phone saved while it was on the same Wi-Fi. An AquaWiz is read through its maker's cloud, so it keeps updating either way, though from there only every 12 hours. Readings you log by hand and the tank's history are always there.

### Can I control equipment when I'm away from home? {#can-i-control-equipment-away}

Yes, if a Cora Max at the tank is online and can reach that equipment. Your command goes to Cora Cloud and the Cora Max carries it out. Without a Cora Max, you can control a Neptune Apex, Red Sea or Jecod device only while your phone is on its network. More in [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device).

### Can two people use the same tank? {#can-two-people-use-the-same-tank}

There's no shared access between two separate accounts yet. A household can sign in to the same Cora account on more than one phone. Everyone sees the same tanks, gets the same notifications, and changes apply to the whole account. For a tank sitter, [Going away](/help/mobile-vacation) makes a read-only page that needs no account. A [shared snapshot](/help/mobile-sharing) is only a picture.

### How do I add another tank? {#how-do-i-add-another-tank}

In Cora Mobile, open **Settings** and tap **Add tank**. Then move its devices to it, and add it to your Cora Max if you have one. More in [Managing your tanks](/help/mobile-tanks).

### I removed a tank by mistake. Can I get it back? {#can-i-get-a-removed-tank-back}

Yes. Removing a tank only hides it. Open **Settings → Removed tanks** and tap **Restore**. Its readings, journal and devices come back as they were. If a removed tank still sends alerts, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#alerts-from-a-removed-tank).

## Equipment

### What equipment does Cora work with? {#what-equipment-does-cora-work-with}

Neptune Apex (with DŌS and Trident), Red Sea ReefBeat equipment, Jecod and Jebao pumps and the AquaWiz KH Controller. For anything else, you can log readings by hand. Every brand, with links to its pages, is in [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).



### Is Cora affiliated with Neptune, Red Sea or Jebao? {#is-cora-affiliated}

No. Cora isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any manufacturer it connects to. Cora talks to their public accounts, official APIs or local network protocols. A manufacturer's own app or firmware update can change or break that connection at any time, without notice. More in [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections).

### My phone found nothing when I searched for equipment. Why? {#search-found-nothing}

Check that the equipment is powered on and already set up in the manufacturer's app, if it needs one. Then check that your phone allows Local Network and Bluetooth access for Cora. Cora can't find equipment that has never joined your network. More in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#a-device-cannot-be-reached).

### Is it safe to let Cora control my dosing pump? {#is-it-safe-to-let-cora-dose}

Cora only doses what you set up: a product, an amount and a schedule you define, within the limits you set for each head. If a dose stops partway, Cora raises an alert saying so, and tells you how much went in when it can. Read it before you assume the full dose went in. More in [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control) and [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#a-dose-stopped-partway-through-and-a-restore-alert-appeared).

### How does Cora know how strong my dosing product is? {#how-strong-is-my-dosing-product}

It comes from the built-in product library, which lists salts and dosing products with their published figures. If you add your own product, you enter the strength yourself. More in [Dosing](/help/mobile-dosing).

## Alerts and notifications

### Why am I not getting any notifications? {#why-no-notifications}

Usually a category is switched off in **Settings → Notifications**, or your phone isn't allowing notifications from Cora. The steps are in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#im-not-getting-notifications).

### Why is my widget grey? {#why-is-my-widget-grey}

Cora isn't grading it: the alert for it is off, it has no range, or the reading is too old. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#a-widget-is-grey).

### Can I stop notifications at night? {#can-i-stop-notifications-at-night}

Cora Mobile has no quiet hours of its own. Use your phone's Focus or Do Not Disturb, and let Cora through if you still want urgent alerts. On Cora Max, **Night dimming** dims the screen at night, and you can turn **Alert sound** off under **SOUND & VOICE** in **Settings → Cora Max Settings**. More in [Notifications](/help/mobile-notifications#quiet-at-night).

### How do I quiet an alert for a while without turning it off? {#how-do-i-silence-an-alert}

On Cora Max, tap **Snooze**. It mutes that alert on that screen for the rule's **Cooldown between alerts** (up to 1 week). The alert stays open and its range doesn't change. On your phone, set a longer **Cooldown between alerts** on the rule instead. More in [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

### What's the difference between Snooze and Dismiss? {#snooze-or-dismiss}

Both are on Cora Max and only affect that screen. **Snooze** mutes the alert there for a while, and it comes back if the problem is still there when the snooze ends. **Dismiss** closes it there until the reading is back in range. If the problem comes back later, you get a new alert.

### Why did I get an alert on my phone but not on Cora Max, or the other way round? {#alert-on-phone-not-on-cora-max}

The switches in **Settings → Notifications** belong to your account, so they apply to every phone you're signed in on. Cora Max shows every alert unless on-screen alerts are turned off for that screen (on your phone, **Devices**, your Cora Max, **Alerts**), or you snoozed or dismissed it there. More in [Alerts on Cora Max](/help/max-alerts).

## Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant

### What is Reef Buddy, and how is it different from the Assistant? {#reef-buddy-or-assistant}

Reef Buddy is your daily briefing, written for you without you asking. It has a headline, two scores and the points behind them. Cora Assistant is what you ask questions, whenever you like, by typing or talking. More in [Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-reef-buddy).

### Why did my briefing stop, or why doesn't my second tank get one? {#why-did-my-briefing-stop}

Reef Buddy is part of the paid plans and the 30-day trial. Each plan covers a set number of tanks, counted in the order of **Settings → Reorder tanks**. On a day when nothing changed, the briefing stays quiet. More in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#my-daily-briefing-stopped).

### What can I ask Cora, and what can it do? {#what-can-i-ask-cora}

Ask about your tank's readings, history, dosing, lab results and journal. Cora can also do things for you, like log a test or switch equipment, and asks you to confirm first. It tells you when it has no data or connection for something, instead of guessing. More in [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant) and [Talking to Cora](/help/max-voice).

### Does Cora remember what I told it last time? {#does-cora-remember}

Yes, within the limits on the [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant) page. It can use earlier context about your tank, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

### Why did Cora give me a wrong or strange answer? {#why-a-wrong-answer}

Cora answers from the data it has for the tank you selected. The usual causes are the wrong tank, missing readings, or a question that could mean two things. Check the tank picker or rephrase, and try again.

## Languages

### Which languages does Cora speak? {#which-languages}

English, German, French, Turkish, Italian, Spanish and Polish. Cora uses one language for the whole account, so everyone signed in to that account sees the same language on every device. More in [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings).

### I changed the language on my phone. Why did my Cora Max change too? {#language-changed-everywhere}

Language is an account setting. Change it on your phone or any Cora Max and it changes everywhere within moments.

### Will old reports and alerts be translated? {#are-old-reports-translated}

No. Anything already written stays in its original language. New alerts, reports and Reef Buddy briefings use the new language.

## Account, plans and privacy

### What's free, and what needs a plan? {#whats-free}

See [Plans and subscriptions](/help/mobile-plans) for what each plan includes.

### Is the Expert plan the same as Cora Max? {#max-plan-or-cora-max}

No. The **Expert** plan is a subscription in Cora Mobile. Cora Max is the wall screen. You don't need one for the other.

### What happens to my tank history if my trial or plan ends? {#history-when-plan-ends}

Nothing is deleted. Your tanks, devices and history stay with your account whatever plan you're on. Only the features you can use change.

### Does deleting my Cora account cancel my Apple or Google subscription? {#does-deleting-cancel-subscription}

No. Cancel the subscription with Apple or Google as well. The **Delete Account** confirmation has a **Manage subscription** button for that. More in [Exporting your data and deleting your account](/help/mobile-data-export).

### How do I change my email or password? {#change-email-or-password}

To change your password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. To change your email after you've verified it, contact support. If you sign in with Apple or Google, change these with Apple or Google. More in [Signing in and account recovery](/help/mobile-account).

### What does Cora do with my tank data? {#what-does-cora-do-with-my-data}

Cora uses it to show your readings, run alerts, and power Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant. What's stored and how it may be used is in [Exporting your data and deleting your account](/help/mobile-data-export), [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings) and the privacy policy.

### Can I get a copy of everything Cora has stored about my tank? {#can-i-get-a-copy}

Yes, you can export it from Cora Mobile. [Exporting your data](/help/mobile-data-export) explains what the export includes.
