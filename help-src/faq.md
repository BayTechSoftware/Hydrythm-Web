## Getting started

### What do I need to buy to use Cora?

Just a phone is enough to start: Cora Mobile runs your tanks, devices and alerts on its own. A Cora Max is an optional wall screen for the reef room, not a requirement.

More: [Cora Help](/help/)

### Do I need a Cora Max tablet, or can I use just the phone app?

The phone app on its own. Every core feature (tanks, devices, dashboards, alerts, dosing) lives in Cora Mobile first, and Cora Max simply shows the same tanks on a bigger screen with voice added.

More: [Cora Help](/help/), [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup)

### How do I add my first tank?

Cora Mobile walks you through it: create your account, then add a tank and answer its profile questions. Devices and dashboards come after the tank exists.

More: [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup)

### What does "tank profile" mean and why does it ask so many questions?

The tank profile is the basic description of your system: size, water type, livestock, and how you dose and test. Cora uses those answers to set sensible starting thresholds and to make Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant answers specific to your tank rather than generic.

More: [Tank profile](/help/mobile-tank-profile)

### How long does setup take?

A single tank with no equipment yet takes a few minutes. Adding real devices (probes, controllers, pumps) takes longer only because each one needs its own pairing step; you can always add more later.

More: [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup)

## Cora Max

### Can one Cora Max show more than one tank?

Yes. A single Cora Max can show every tank on your account, one at a time, and the Reef Room view shows them all together.

More: [The Reef Room](/help/max-reef-room)

### What is "Primary Cora Max" and do I need to set it?

Primary Cora Max decides which tablet talks to a tank's local equipment. Most people never touch it: with one tablet there is nothing to choose, and the default, **Any active (automatic)**, lets every online Cora Max share the job automatically. Pin a specific tablet only if you want just one device doing the polling.

More: [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device)

### Which device answers "Hey Cora" if I have more than one Cora Max?

Only one Cora device answers the wake phrase at a time, chosen in Settings under **Cora Assistant**. This is a separate choice from Primary Cora Max: one is about voice, the other is about talking to your equipment.

More: [Voice on Cora Max](/help/max-voice), [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device)

### Does Cora Max need the internet to work, or just my home Wi-Fi?

Cora Max needs your home Wi-Fi to reach your equipment, and it needs an internet connection to reach Cora Cloud for sign-in, sync and the Assistant. The status pill in the top bar tells you which one it currently has.

More: [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour)

### Does Cora Max keep working if Cora Cloud is unreachable?

Cora Max keeps showing the last data it has and keeps working with equipment on its own network, but sign-in, sync between devices, and the Assistant need Cora Cloud back before they work again.

More: [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour), [Devices and device health](/help/max-devices)

### Can I move my Cora Max to a different Wi-Fi network later?

Yes, from the Cora Max itself: join the new network under its own network settings. Your tanks, devices and account stay the same; only the Wi-Fi connection changes.

More: [Setting up Cora Max](/help/max-setup)

## Phone app

### Why does Cora Mobile ask for Local Network and Bluetooth access?

Cora Mobile needs Local Network access to find and pair a Cora Max or Wi-Fi equipment on your home network, and Bluetooth access for equipment that pairs that way. Without them, pairing simply cannot find the device.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Can two Cora Max tablets show the same tank?

Yes. Add both to the same tank and each shows the same live data; Primary Cora Max only decides which one talks to the local equipment, not which one can display the tank.

More: [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device)

### Can I check on my tank from my phone when I'm not home?

Yes, readings and history are always available through Cora Cloud, wherever you are. A few device-specific settings (as opposed to readings) only open while your phone is on the same network as the device itself.

More: [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device)

### Can I control equipment, like turning off a pump, when I'm away from home?

For most equipment, yes: outlets, feed mode and similar controls go through Cora Cloud, so they work away from your tank's network. A few device-native settings still need your phone on the tank's own Wi-Fi.

More: [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device), [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control)

### Can two people use the same tank?

Cora is built around one account per tank today. If you want another person to see or help with a tank, sharing it with them is the supported route rather than logging into the same account on two phones.

More: [Sharing](/help/mobile-sharing)

## Devices and integrations

### What equipment does Cora work with?

Cora integrates with a growing list of controllers and equipment, from Wi-Fi controllers to dosing pumps and gyres. The current list is on the connections page, and Cora also supports hand-logged readings for anything it does not connect to directly.

More: [Connections](/help/mobile-connections)

### Is Cora affiliated with Neptune, Red Sea, Jebao, or Maxspect?

No. Cora is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any equipment manufacturer it connects to, including Maxspect, whose integration is still in beta. Cora talks to their public accounts or local network protocols, and a manufacturer's own app or firmware update can change or break that connection at any time, on their side, without notice.

More: [Connections](/help/mobile-connections)

### Why can't Cora Mobile find my Cora Max when I try to pair it?

Almost always Wi-Fi: your phone and the Cora Max need to be on the same network for pairing, and on iPhone, Cora also needs Local Network access. Cora Mobile shows which pairing step failed and gives you a **Retry**.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi as my Cora Max?

To pair it, yes. Once paired, day-to-day use works from anywhere through Cora Cloud; only a handful of device-native settings need the phone back on that Wi-Fi.

More: [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### What happens if I change my Wi-Fi router or network name?

Any equipment or Cora Max that connected over that network will stop being reachable until it rejoins the new network or name. Rejoin the device the same way you first added it.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### My device found nothing when I searched for equipment, what's wrong?

Check the equipment is powered on and already set up in its own manufacturer app if it needs one, and that your phone has Local Network and Bluetooth access allowed. Equipment that has never joined your network cannot be found until it does.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Why did my Red Sea device's readings stop updating?

Cora shows a banner saying no device is currently reading it when nothing on that tank's network is currently polling your Red Sea equipment. Check Primary Cora Max in Settings, or open the tank on a device that is on the same Wi-Fi as the Red Sea gear.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting), [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device)

### Why does it say "Could not reach the pump" when I try to control my Jecod pump?

The command never reached the pump, usually because it is off, off its network, or (for a Bluetooth-only pump) out of range. Check its power and connection, move closer if it pairs over Bluetooth, then try again.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Why does the gyre program not apply?

A schedule push to a gyre can fail the same way a command can: the gyre did not respond. Check it is powered and on its network, then retry from the schedule screen.

More: [Scheduling equipment](/help/mobile-schedules), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Is it safe to let Cora control my dosing pump automatically?

Cora only doses what you set up: a product, a strength and a schedule you define. If a dose is interrupted partway, Cora raises a specific alert about it rather than assuming it completed, so nothing is silently under- or over-dosed.

More: [Dosing](/help/mobile-dosing), [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control)

### What happens if a dose is interrupted halfway through?

Cora raises an alert telling you a dose did not finish and, where it can tell, how much actually went in. Check that alert before assuming the full dose was delivered, and resume or adjust from there.

More: [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control)

### How does Cora know how strong my dosing product is?

Either from a built-in product library of sourced salts and dosing products with published figures, or from strength you enter yourself when you add your own product.

More: [Dosing](/help/mobile-dosing)

## Alerts and notifications

### Why am I not getting notifications at all?

Check the alert's category is allowed to push under **Settings → Notifications**, and check your phone's own notification permission for Cora. The daily briefing is also deliberately quiet on days when nothing changed, which is expected, not a fault.

More: [Notifications](/help/mobile-notifications), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Why is an alert not clearing even though the reading looks fine?

Either the reading is genuinely still out of range (check its history), the threshold does not fit your tank, or the source itself (often a probe needing calibration) is reporting a number that is out of range. Fixing the source is usually the right move, not the threshold.

More: [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### How do I silence an alert temporarily without turning it off?

Use **Snooze**. It mutes that alert for a period you choose without closing it or changing the threshold that triggered it.

More: [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts)

### What's the difference between snoozing an alert and dismissing it?

**Dismiss** closes the alert for good, for when you have seen it and accepted the situation. **Snooze** only mutes it for a while; the alert can come back once the snooze period ends if the condition is still there.

More: [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts)

### Can I set how long an alert stays snoozed, is a week the longest?

Yes, you choose the length when you snooze, and a week is the longest cooldown Cora offers.

More: [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts)

### Why did I get an alert on my phone but not on Cora Max, or vice versa?

Each app and each Cora Max has its own notification settings, so a category allowed on one may be off on the other. Check **Settings → Notifications** on both.

More: [Notifications](/help/mobile-notifications), [Alerts on Cora Max](/help/max-alerts)

## Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant

### What can I ask Cora, and what can't it do?

Cora Assistant answers questions about your tank's readings, history, dosing and journal, and can carry out equipment actions you ask for by voice or text. It cannot act on anything it has no data or connection for, and it will say so rather than guess.

More: [The Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant)

### Can Cora actually control my equipment, or just tell me things?

Both. Ask it a question and it answers from your tank's data; ask it to do something (like starting feed mode) and, if the equipment supports it, it carries that out.

More: [The Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant), [Voice on Cora Max](/help/max-voice)

### Does Cora remember what I told it last time?

Within the limits described on the Assistant page, yes: it can use earlier context from your tank so you don't have to repeat yourself every time.

More: [The Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant)

### Why did Cora give me a wrong or strange answer?

Cora Assistant answers from the data it has for the tank you have selected; a wrong tank picked, missing readings, or an ambiguous question are the usual causes. Rephrase, or check the tank picker, and try again.

More: [The Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant)

### What is Reef Buddy and how is it different from the Assistant?

Reef Buddy is your daily briefing, written for you without being asked: a headline, two scores and the insights behind them. Cora Assistant is what you ask questions of, on demand, by typing or voice.

More: [Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-reef-buddy), [Glossary](/help/glossary)

## Languages

### What language does Cora speak, and can family members use a different one?

Cora uses one language for the whole account, not a separate one per person or per device. Everyone signed into that account sees the same language everywhere.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings), [Glossary](/help/glossary)

### I changed the language on my phone, why did my Cora Max change too?

Because language is an account setting, not a device setting. Changing it anywhere (phone or any Cora Max) changes it everywhere within moments.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings), [Glossary](/help/glossary)

### Will old reports and alerts be translated after I change languages?

No, and that is expected. Anything already generated stays in the language it was written in; only new alerts, reports and Reef Buddy briefings from that point on use the new language.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings), [Glossary](/help/glossary)

## Account, plans and privacy

### What's free forever, and what needs a subscription?

The current plans, their limits and what each one includes are listed on the Plans page inside the app and on the Plans help page; check there rather than relying on a number here, since plans can change.

More: [Plans](/help/mobile-plans)

### What happens to my data if my trial or subscription ends?

Your tank history is not deleted when a trial or subscription ends. What changes is which features remain available; see the Plans page for exactly what each tier includes.

More: [Plans](/help/mobile-plans)

### If I delete my Cora account, does that cancel my Apple or Google subscription?

No. Deleting your Cora account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play; cancel that separately through Apple or Google.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings)

### Can I change or cancel my plan without losing my tank history?

Yes, your tanks, devices and history stay with your account regardless of plan changes; only which features you can use changes.

More: [Plans](/help/mobile-plans)

### What does Cora do with my tank data?

Cora uses your tank data to show you your readings, drive alerts and thresholds, and power Reef Buddy and Cora Assistant. Details of what is stored and how it may be used are on the data export and settings pages, and in the privacy policy.

More: [Your data](/help/mobile-data-export), [Settings](/help/mobile-settings)

### Can I get a copy of everything Cora has stored about my tank?

Yes, an export is available from the app; see the data export page for what it includes and how to request it.

More: [Your data](/help/mobile-data-export)

### If I delete my account, is everything really gone?

Deleting your account removes your data as described on the Settings page, including the option around contributing anonymised data. Read that page before deleting if you want the exact detail.

More: [Settings](/help/mobile-settings)

## Troubleshooting pointers

### Why does a widget show no value?

Usually a connection problem, not a missing parameter: check nearby widgets for the same issue, then check the device's row under **Devices**.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Why does a reading say it's from an hour ago?

The age badge is accurate: nothing newer has arrived. Some equipment is genuinely slow by design (an hourly titrator, for example); anything else, check the device's row under **Devices**.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### My device says "sign-in refused", what do I do?

The manufacturer's account rejected the stored sign-in, almost always because the password changed on their side. Open the device row and sign in again.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)

### Why does Cora Max show old data even though it says "Online"?

**Online** and **Cloud** both mean the screen itself is healthy; if the pill reads either of those and the data is still old, the problem is upstream of Cora Max, not the screen. Check the same tank on your phone.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting), [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour)

### Why do my probe and my test kit disagree?

That is Cora reporting a real disagreement, not a fault. Calibrate the probe, retest with fresh reagent, and compare both under the same conditions; an ICP result can add a third data point.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting), [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health)

### What happens if my internet goes down?

Cora Max and Cora Mobile keep showing the last data they received and keep working with equipment already on their own network. Sign-in, sync and the Assistant come back once the connection returns.

More: [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting), [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour)

### Does Cora work without internet?

Partly. Readings that were already delivered, and control of equipment already on the same local network, keep working. Sign-in, cross-device sync, Cora Cloud storage and the Assistant all need an internet connection.

More: [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour), [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting)
