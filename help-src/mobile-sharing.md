Cora can make a **tank snapshot**. It's one card with your tank's current parameters, laid out for sharing.

## Creating one

Tap the **share** icon in the tank header on the dashboard. Cora builds the card and shows you a preview before anything is sent.

![The tank snapshot sheet](img/mobile-sharing.webp "Choose which metrics appear, and the shape of the card.")

## What it contains

The card shows your tank's name, age, type and livestock count. Below that are the metrics you picked, each with its current value and recent trend.

## Choosing what appears

**Metrics on the card** shows every parameter the tank tracks as a chip. Tick the ones you want, or tap **Select all**. Three or four look best. Any more and the card gets cramped.

## Shape

There are two formats.

- **Square (1:1)** is for a post or a message.
- **Story (9:16)** is for a full-screen story.

## Sending it

**Share** opens your phone's usual share sheet, so you can send the card anywhere your phone can send a picture. Nothing leaves your account until you pick where it goes.

:::note A snapshot doesn't give access
Sharing a snapshot doesn't let anyone into your account, your history or your equipment. If someone's looking after the tank while you're away, use [Going away](/help/mobile-vacation). It makes a read-only page for them.
:::
