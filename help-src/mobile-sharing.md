Cora can make a **tank snapshot**: one picture of your tank's current parameters, laid out for social media or a message to a friend.

## Creating one {#creating-one}

Tap the share icon in the tank header on the dashboard. You can also tap the camera icon at the top of a Health Report. **Tank Snapshot** opens with a preview of the card, and nothing is sent until you choose where it goes.

![The tank snapshot sheet](img/mobile-sharing.webp "Choose which metrics appear, and the shape of the card.")

## What it contains {#what-it-contains}

The card shows your tank's name, age, type and livestock count, plus its health score when there's a recent one. Below that are the metrics you picked, each with its current value and recent trend.

## Choosing what appears {#choosing-what-appears}

**Metrics on the card** shows a chip for every parameter with readings in the last week. Tap chips to add or remove them. **Select all** turns them all on, and once they're all on the button becomes **Reset**, which goes back to the starting choice. Three or four look best. Any more and the card gets cramped. The card always keeps at least one.

This phone remembers your choice for that tank next time.

## Shape {#shape}

There are two formats.

- **Square** (1:1) is for a post or a message.
- **Story** (9:16) is for a full-screen story.

## Sending it {#sending-it}

**Share** opens your phone's usual share sheet, so you can send the card anywhere your phone can send a picture. Nothing leaves your account until you pick where it goes.

:::note A snapshot doesn't give access
A snapshot is only a picture. It doesn't let anyone into your account, your history or your equipment. If someone's looking after the tank while you're away, use [Going away](/help/mobile-vacation). It makes a read-only page for them.
:::
