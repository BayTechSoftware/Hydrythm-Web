Cora can produce a **tank snapshot** — a single card showing your tank's current parameters, formatted for sharing.

## Creating one

Tap the **share** icon in the tank header on the dashboard. Cora builds the card and shows a preview before anything is sent.

## What it contains

The tank name, its current readings, and when they were taken. It is a picture of the tank at that moment, not a live link — the recipient sees the values as they were when you shared it.

## Where it goes

The preview hands the card to your phone's normal share sheet, so it can go anywhere your phone can send an image.

:::note A snapshot is not access
Sharing a snapshot gives no one access to your account, your history or your equipment. To let someone look after the tank while you are away, use [Going away](/help/mobile-vacation) instead, which produces a read-only page.
:::
