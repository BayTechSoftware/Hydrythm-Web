Cora Max shows the same intelligence as the phone, on a screen large enough to read from a distance. Reach these from the **tank menu** — tap the tank name in the top bar.

![Reef Buddy on Cora Max](img/max-intelligence.webp "The briefing at full size.")

## Reef Buddy

The daily briefing appears on the dashboard and has its own full screen: the Stability and Data scores, the summary, and the insights behind them.

See [Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-reef-buddy) for what the scores mean.

## ICP results

![ICP reports on Cora Max](img/max-icp.webp "Every uploaded result, with its score.")

Your uploaded results, with the score and summary for each, and trends across every report.

Uploading a result is done from the phone — see [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## Health reports

Run and read a health report at the wall. It is the same assessment the phone produces.

## History charts

Any parameter can be opened as a full-screen chart, over the same ranges as the phone. On a wall display this is the most useful way to look at a slow drift — a month of alkalinity at full screen shows a trend that a small tile cannot.

## Guided journeys

You can follow a journey on Cora Max and see where it has got to.

**A cycling journey also takes readings here** — **Log a test** on the cycling screen records ammonia, nitrite and nitrate without going to your phone, which is the point when you are standing at the tank with a test kit.

An **issue** journey is read-only at the wall: starting one, completing a step, recording an outcome, and resolving or dismissing it are done on the phone. See [Guided journeys](/help/mobile-journeys).
