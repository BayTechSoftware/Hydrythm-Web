The words you'll meet in Cora Mobile and on Cora Max, in alphabetical order. Each one links to the page that explains it in full.

## Account language

The one language your whole Cora account uses: English, German, French, Turkish, Italian, Spanish or Polish. It isn't set per person or per device. Change it in **Settings → Language** on your phone or on any Cora Max, and every device follows within moments. Anything already written stays in its original language. See [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings).

## Age

How long ago a reading was taken, shown on every parameter widget as `now`, `1h`, `2d`. It isn't when the screen last refreshed. An old age means the reading itself is old.

## Alert

Cora telling you something needs attention. Usually it's a reading outside the range you set. It can also be a parameter moving too fast, an equipment fault, a device that stopped reporting, a consumable running low, or a lab result coming in. Most alerts clear by themselves once the problem passes. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Answering device

The Cora device that answers when you say "Hey Cora". You pick it in **Settings → Cora Assistant**. Only one device answers at a time. It's a different setting from [Primary Cora Max](#primary-cora-max).

## AquaWiz

The AquaWiz KH Controller, an alkalinity tester. Cora reads it through AquaWiz's own cloud. See [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

## Automation

A rule that runs by itself, like "if temperature goes above 27, turn on the fan". Each rule has a [trigger](#trigger), optional [conditions](#condition) and one or more actions. Find them in Cora Mobile under **Settings → Automations**. See [Automations and scenes](/help/mobile-automation).


## Calibration

Adjusting a probe so its readings match a known reference, usually a calibration solution. You can record when you calibrated or cleaned a probe. See [Parameter Mapping and probes](/help/mobile-probes).

## Child lock

A Cora Max setting that stops anyone switching equipment from that screen, by touch or by voice, after a set time with no touch. Readings and questions still work. See [Cora Max settings](/help/max-settings#child-lock).

## Command outcome

How a command to your equipment ended. **Confirmed**: the equipment reported back that it did it. **Unconfirmed**: sent, but nothing reported back, so check the device. **Refused**: it didn't run. **No change**: it was already that way. See [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control).

## Condition

An extra check on an [automation](#automation), such as a reading being above a value. The actions only run when the conditions are met. See [Automations and scenes](/help/mobile-automation).

## Cooldown

The shortest time Cora waits before sending the same alert again, from 15 minutes to a week. It keeps one ongoing problem from pinging you every few minutes. You set it for each alert rule as **Cooldown between alerts**. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Cora Assistant

The part of Cora you ask questions and ask to do things. You can type or talk to it in Cora Mobile, and talk to it on Cora Max. It's also the name of the settings group where you choose the [answering device](#answering-device) and data sharing. See [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant).

## Cora Cloud

The service that keeps your phone, your Cora Max and your equipment in step, and stores your history. Syncing between devices and the Assistant need it. Readings you already have, and equipment on its own network, keep working without it.

## Cora Max

The wall screen for the reef room. It shows your tanks live, reads your equipment around the clock, and you can talk to it. See [Setting up Cora Max](/help/max-setup).

## Cora Mobile

The phone app. The icon on your home screen says **Cora**. See [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup).

## Design with AI

A button in the dashboard editor. Cora proposes a whole layout from what the tank reports and has linked. It works on a paid plan and during the trial, and needs AI data sharing on. **Templates** are ready-made layouts to start from. See [Editing the Cora Max dashboard](/help/max-dashboard-editing).

## Dissolved CO₂

CO₂ in the water, in mg/L, as an ICP report gives it. It isn't the CO₂ of the air in your room. See [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## DŌS head

One pump of a Neptune DŌS doser. You can give each head a use (a water change, kalkwasser, a reactor, feeding and more), track it in litres or large containers, and set its own limits. If a head loses contact mid-dose, Cora raises a restore alert. See [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control).

## Dose

One delivery of liquid from a DŌS head or another dosing pump, using the product and amount you set. Cora logs every dose so your consumption and history match up.

## Feed mode

A pause for feeding. On a Neptune Apex, **Feed** starts one of the feed cycles, **A** to **D**, programmed on the Apex, and the Apex decides what pauses and when it comes back. A Jecod pump can pause for feeding from its own page. Some Jecod pumps can't end the pause by themselves: keep the pump's page open on your phone until the pause ends, or the pump stays stopped. See [Outlets and controls](/help/max-controls) and [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control).

## Feeding routine

How you usually feed, set once for the tank (for example, flakes twice a day). After that you only log the days that differ, and Reef Buddy takes both into account. See [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).



## Guided journey

A step-by-step plan Cora walks you through, such as cycling a new tank or working through a problem. See [Guided journeys](/help/mobile-journeys).

## Health report

A deeper, periodic look at a whole system. It takes every parameter, source, dose and recent lab result into account together. See [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).


## ICP

A lab water test that covers a wide range of elements. Upload the PDF your lab sends, or have the lab send it to Cora, and Cora tracks every element over time. See [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## Insight

One specific point in a Reef Buddy briefing, such as a parameter drifting, two sources disagreeing, or something you haven't tested in a while.

## Jecod

Jecod and Jebao pumps. Cora reads and controls them over your network or Bluetooth. See [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

## Journal

Your record of what you did to the tank. Cora reads it too, so you can ask "what did I do before the alkalinity dropped?" and get an answer. See [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).

## Neptune Apex

The Neptune Systems controller, with its DŌS doser and Trident tester. Cora reads it on your home network. See [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

## Outlet

A switchable socket on your controller. In Cora it has three states: **Auto**, **Off** and **On**. See [Outlets and controls](/help/max-controls).

## Parameter

Something you can measure in your water, like alkalinity, calcium, nitrate or temperature.

## Parameter Mapping

Where you tell Cora what each probe on a controller measures, and which device feeds each reading. See [Parameter Mapping and probes](/help/mobile-probes).

## Primary Cora Max

The Cora Max that reads a tank's equipment on your network for you. Set it to one Cora Max, or to **Any active (automatic)**, where every online Cora Max for the tank shares the job. It's about reading equipment, not voice (that's the [answering device](#answering-device)). Change it in Cora Mobile: **Settings**, open the tank, then **Primary Cora Max**. See [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device).

## Recovery PIN

A six-digit number Cora Max shows at the end of setup. It's the only way into recovery on that Cora Max. You can also find it with the Cora Max's details in Cora Mobile. See [Updates and recovery](/help/max-updates).

## Red Sea ReefBeat

Red Sea's family of connected equipment: ReefDose, ReefRun, ReefMat and ReefATO+. Cora reads it on your home network. See [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

## Reef Buddy

Your daily briefing. It has a headline, two scores (**Stability** out of 100 and **Data** as a percentage), and the insights behind them. It's part of the paid plans. See [Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-reef-buddy).

## Reef Room

The Cora Max screen that shows all your tanks side by side. On screen it's called **Cora Tanks**. See [The Reef Room](/help/max-reef-room).

## Removed tank

A tank you removed. Its data is kept, and you can bring it back from **Settings → Removed tanks**. See [Managing your tanks](/help/mobile-tanks). It is never shown, sends no alerts or reminders, and its automations stop.


## Saved design

A Cora Max dashboard layout you've kept under **My dashboards**. You can apply it again later or load it onto another tank or Cora Max. The phone dashboard has no saved designs. See [Editing the Cora Max dashboard](/help/max-dashboard-editing#saving-layouts).

## Scene

A named group of actions, like "Water change" or "Night". You can run it when you want, on a schedule, or from inside another [automation](#automation). Scenes are shared by Cora Mobile and Cora Max. See [Automations and scenes](/help/mobile-automation).


## Snooze

A button on a Cora Max alert. It mutes the alert on that Cora Max for the rule's cooldown (up to a week) without closing it. **Dismiss**, the other button, closes it on that Cora Max until the reading is back in range. Your phone's notifications aren't affected by either. See [Alerts on Cora Max](/help/max-alerts).

## Source

Where a reading came from: a probe, a controller, a lab result, or you with a test kit. Every reading has one, so Cora can tell you when two sources disagree. If more than one source reports the same reading, [Parameter Mapping](/help/mobile-probes) is where you choose which one to trust, or leave it Automatic.

## Status dot

The coloured dot on each row of the Devices tab in Cora Mobile. Green is working, orange is a warning or fault, red is offline and grey means Cora doesn't know yet. See [Adding, editing and removing devices](/help/mobile-devices).

## Status tag

The tag in the Cora Max top bar that says how this screen is getting its data, such as **Online**, **Cloud**, **Stale** or **Offline**. See [The Cora Max home screen](/help/max-tour).

## Tank

A body of water you track. A frag system or a quarantine tank counts as a separate tank. See [Your tank profile](/help/mobile-tank-profile).

## Threshold

The range you're happy with for a parameter. It can be a range, a ceiling or a floor. You set it, and the defaults are only a starting point. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Trident

Neptune's tester for alkalinity, calcium and magnesium. It runs with a Neptune Apex. See [Equipment by brand](/help/equipment-by-brand).

## Trigger

What wakes an [automation](#automation): a parameter crossing a value, an alert, a time of day, or a device going offline or coming back. See [Automations and scenes](/help/mobile-automation).

## Vacation mode

Tank-sitter mode. You make a dated plan with a checklist of jobs, and Cora turns it into a read-only page you can share with whoever looks after the tank while you're away. See [Going away](/help/mobile-vacation).

## Wake word

"Hey Cora", the phrase that starts a voice conversation on Cora Max. You can turn it off on each Cora Max and tap the Cora icon instead. See [Talking to Cora](/help/max-voice).

## Widget

One tile on a dashboard that shows one thing, such as a value, a gauge, a graph or an outlet. See [Widget reference](/help/mobile-widgets).
