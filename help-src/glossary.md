## Account language

The one language your whole Cora account uses. It isn't set per person or per device. Change it in **Settings → Language** on your phone or on any Cora Max, and every device follows within moments. New alerts, reports and Reef Buddy briefings use the new language from then on. Anything already written stays in its original language. See [Settings](/help/mobile-settings).

## Age

How long ago a reading was taken, shown on every parameter widget as `now`, `1h`, `2d`. It has nothing to do with when the screen last refreshed. An old age means the reading itself is old.

## Alert

Cora telling you something needs attention. Usually it's a reading outside the range you set. It can also be a parameter moving too fast, an equipment fault, a consumable running low, or a lab result coming in. Most alerts clear by themselves once the problem passes. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Answering device

The Cora device that answers when you say the wake phrase. You pick it in **Settings → Cora Assistant**. Only one device answers at a time. **Primary Cora Max** is a different setting. It decides which device talks to your local equipment, and has nothing to do with voice.

## Automation

A rule or scene that runs by itself, like "if temperature goes above 27, turn on the fan". You'll find these in **Settings → Automation**. On newer Cora Max builds you can also build and edit a scene on Cora Max itself.

## Calibration

Adjusting a probe so its readings match a known reference, usually a calibration solution. Cora records when you last calibrated a probe and can remind you when it's due. See [Probes](/help/mobile-probes).

## Cooldown

The shortest time Cora waits before sending the same alert again, from 15 minutes to a week. It keeps one ongoing problem from pinging you every few minutes. You set it for each alert rule. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Cora Cloud

The service that keeps your phone, your Cora Max and your equipment in step, and stores your history. Sign-in, syncing between devices and the Assistant all need it. Readings you already have, and equipment already on its own network, keep working without it.

## Cora Assistant

The part of Cora you ask questions. You can type or talk to it on both Cora Mobile and Cora Max. It's also the name of the settings group where you choose the [answering device](#answering-device).

## Cora Max

The wall screen for the reef room. It shows your tanks live and you can talk to it.

## Cora Mobile

The phone app. The icon on your home screen says **Cora**.

## DŌS head

A dosing pump connected to Cora. You can give each head a use (a water change, kalkwasser, a reactor, feeding and more), track it in litres or large containers, and run large-volume doses. If a head loses contact mid-dose, Cora raises a restore alert. It doesn't assume the dose finished. See [Controlling your equipment](/help/mobile-device-control).

## Dose

One delivery of liquid from a DŌS head or another dosing pump, using the product and amount you set. Cora logs every dose so your consumption and history match up.

## Feed mode

A pause for feeding. It stops the right equipment and turns it back on automatically. That's safer than switching pumps off by hand, because you don't have to remember to switch them back on.

## Health report

A deeper, periodic look at a whole system. It takes every parameter, source, dose and recent lab result into account together. See [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## ICP

A lab water test that covers a wide range of elements. Upload the result and Cora tracks every element over time.

## Insight

One specific point in a Reef Buddy briefing, such as a parameter drifting, two sources disagreeing, or something you haven't tested in a while.

## Journal

Your record of what you did to the tank. Cora reads it too, so you can ask "what did I do before the alkalinity dropped?" and get an answer. See [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).

## Outlet

A switchable socket on your equipment. In Cora it has three states, **Auto**, **Off** and **On**.

## Parameter

Something you can measure in your water, like alkalinity, calcium, nitrate or temperature.

## Primary Cora Max

The Cora Max that talks to a tank's local equipment (its Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices) for you. Set it to one Cora Max, or to **Any active (automatic)**. With that option every online Cora Max for the tank shares the job, and the last write wins. This is separate from the [answering device](#answering-device), which is about voice. Primary Cora Max is about polling equipment. Change it in **Settings → Primary Cora Max**. See [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device).

## Saved design

A dashboard layout you've kept under **My dashboards**. You can apply it again later or load it onto another tank or screen.

## Reef Buddy

Your daily briefing. It has a headline, two scores (**Stability** out of 100 and **Data** as a percentage), and the insights behind them. See [Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-reef-buddy).

## Snooze

A button on a Cora Max alert. It mutes the alert on that Cora Max for the rule's cooldown (up to a week) without closing it. **Dismiss** is the other button. It closes the alert on that Cora Max until the reading is back in range. Your phone's notifications aren't affected by either. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).

## Source

Where a reading came from, whether that's a probe, a controller, a lab result, or you with a test kit. Every reading has one, so Cora can tell you when two sources disagree.

## Tank

A body of water you track. Most people have one. A frag system or a quarantine tank counts as a separate tank.

## Threshold

The range you're happy with for a parameter. It can be a range, a ceiling or a floor. You set it. The defaults are only a starting point.

## Vacation mode

Tank-sitter mode. You make a dated plan with a checklist of jobs, and Cora turns it into a read-only page you can share with whoever looks after the tank while you're away. See [Settings](/help/mobile-settings).

## Widget

One tile on a dashboard that shows one thing. There are nine types: Value, Gauge, Graph, Status, Outlet, ReefBeat, Apex module, Jecod and Maxspect *(beta)*. See [Widget reference](/help/mobile-widgets).
