Cora Mobile has five tabs along the bottom. Almost everything you do lives in one of them.

![The five tabs of Cora Mobile](img/mobile-tabs.webp "Dashboard, Devices, Assistant, Intelligence, Settings.")

## Dashboard

Your tanks, at a glance. This is the screen you will spend most of your time on.

If you have more than one tank, swipe left and right to move between them — the dots under the header show where you are. The tank header carries the tank name and a row of actions: feed mode, the Reef Buddy briefing, sharing, and the pencil that opens dashboard editing.

Full detail: **[Reading your dashboard](/help/mobile-dashboard)**.

## Devices

Everything you have connected, grouped by brand. Each group collapses, so a reef room full of equipment stays readable.

This is where you add new equipment, rename it, assign it to a tank, and remove it. Full detail: **[Adding, editing and removing devices](/help/mobile-devices)**.

## Assistant

Ask Cora about your tank in plain language, by typing or by voice. It can see your live readings, your history and your ICP results, so "why is my alkalinity falling?" is a question it can actually answer about *your* tank.

Full detail: **[Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant)**.

## Intelligence

Your lab work and your long view. Upload an ICP test and Cora reads it, tracks every element over time, and tells you what moved since last time. Health Reports are a deeper periodic assessment of the whole system.

Full detail: **[ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health)**.

## Settings

Account, tanks, dosing products, the Assistant, notifications, automation, and your plan.

Full detail: **[Settings](/help/mobile-settings)**.

## The bits that aren't tabs

**The bell**, top right, is your notification history — every alert Cora has raised, newest first. The number on it is how many you haven't read.

**The Journal button** floats over the bottom right of the Dashboard. Tap it to write down what you just did — a water change, a new coral, a dose you changed. See **[The journal](/help/mobile-journal)**.
