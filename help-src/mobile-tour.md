Cora Mobile has five tabs along the bottom. Almost everything you do happens in one of them.

![The five tabs of Cora Mobile](img/mobile-tabs.webp "Dashboard, Devices, Assistant, Intelligence, Settings.")

## Dashboard

This is the main screen of Cora Mobile. It shows live readings for the tank you've picked.

If you have more than one tank, swipe left or right to move between them. The dots under the header show where you are. The tank header has the tank name and a row of buttons for feed mode, the Reef Buddy briefing and sharing, plus a pencil that opens the tank profile. To change the dashboard itself, use **Edit dashboard** at the bottom of the page.

You'll find more in [Reading your dashboard](/help/mobile-dashboard).

## Devices

Here's everything you've connected, grouped by brand. You can collapse each group, so even a reef room full of equipment is easy to read.

This is where you add new equipment, rename it, assign it to a tank or remove it. More in [Adding, editing and removing devices](/help/mobile-devices).

## Assistant

Ask Cora about your tank in plain language, by typing or by voice. It can see your live readings, your history and your ICP results. So if you ask "why is my alkalinity falling?", it answers about *your* tank.

More in [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant).

## Intelligence

This is where your lab results live. Upload an ICP test and Cora reads it, tracks every element over time and tells you what's changed since last time. Health Reports go deeper, with a regular check of the whole system.

More in [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## Settings

Here you manage your account, tanks, dosing products, the Assistant, notifications, automation and your plan.

More in [Settings](/help/mobile-settings).

## The first-run tour

The first time you open the dashboard, Cora walks you through the parts of the screen one by one. It only does this once.

To see it again, go to **Settings → About → Replay tips**. The tour restarts and takes you to the dashboard to begin. It's handy after an update, or when you hand your phone to someone else.

## Two controls outside the tabs

**The bell** at the top right holds your notification history, with every alert Cora has raised, newest first. The number on it is how many you haven't read yet.

**The Journal button** floats at the bottom right of the Dashboard. Tap it to note what you just did, like a water change, a new coral or a dose you changed. See [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).
