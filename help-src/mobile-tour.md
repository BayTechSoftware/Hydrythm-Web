Cora Mobile has five tabs along the bottom: **Dashboard**, **Devices**, **Assistant**, **Intelligence** and **Settings**. Almost everything you do happens in one of them.

![The five tabs of Cora Mobile](img/mobile-tabs.webp "Dashboard, Devices, Assistant, Intelligence, Settings.")

## Dashboard {#dashboard}

This is the main screen of Cora Mobile. It shows live readings for one tank.

If you have more than one tank, swipe left or right to move between them. The dots at the top show which one you're on. See [Managing your tanks](/help/mobile-tanks).

The tank header has the tank name, which you tap to rename the tank, and a row of icons:

- **Feed** opens **Feed mode**, where you start one of your Neptune Apex feed cycles. It only shows on a tank with an Apex. See [The tank header](/help/mobile-dashboard#the-tank-header).
- The newspaper icon opens the latest **Reef Buddy** briefing.
- The share icon makes a snapshot of the tank to send. See [Sharing your tank](/help/mobile-sharing).
- The ruler opens **Units**, which sets how this tank shows its readings.
- The pencil opens the [tank profile](/help/mobile-tank-profile).

Just under the widgets is **Edit dashboard**, for changing the dashboard itself. Below it, a row of buttons has **Log Parameters**, **Parameter History** and **Feeding**, then shortcuts to the tank's journal, alerts, maintenance and livestock.

You'll find more in [Reading your dashboard](/help/mobile-dashboard).

## Devices {#devices}

Here's everything you've connected, grouped by brand. You can collapse each group, so even a reef room full of equipment is easy to read.

This is where you add new equipment, rename it, assign it to a tank or remove it. More in [Adding, editing and removing devices](/help/mobile-devices).

## Assistant {#assistant}

Ask Cora about your tank in plain language, by typing or by voice. It can see your live readings, your history and your ICP results. So if you ask "why is my alkalinity falling?", it answers about *your* tank.

More in [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant).

## Intelligence {#intelligence}

This is where your lab results and health reports live. Upload an ICP test and Cora reads it, tracks every element over time and tells you what's changed since last time. A Health Report is a full check of the whole system, and you run one whenever you want it.

More in [ICP and health reports](/help/mobile-icp-health).

## Settings {#settings}

Here you manage your account, your tanks, dosing products, Cora Assistant, notifications, automations and your plan. Tap a tank in the list to open everything that belongs to it, like its alert thresholds, **Parameter Mapping** and **Units**.

More in [Cora Mobile settings](/help/mobile-settings).

## The first-run tour {#the-first-run-tour}

The first time you open the dashboard, Cora points out the parts of the screen one by one: the widgets, **Edit dashboard**, long-pressing a widget to tune its alert range, **Log Parameters**, the shortcuts to your records, and the **Devices**, **Assistant** and **Intelligence** tabs. It only does this once.

To see it again, go to **Settings → About → Replay tips**. The tour restarts and takes you to the dashboard to begin. It's handy after an update, or when you hand your phone to someone else.

## Two controls outside the tabs {#two-controls-outside-the-tabs}

**The bell** at the top right holds your notification history, with every alert Cora has raised, newest first. The number on it is how many you haven't read yet.

**The Journal button** floats at the bottom right of the Dashboard. Tap it and say what you just did, like a water change, a new coral or a dose you changed. Tap the stop button when you're done, and Cora writes it into the journal. See [The journal](/help/mobile-journal).
