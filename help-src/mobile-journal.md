The journal is where you write down what you did to the tank: water changes, new livestock, dosing changes, equipment work and anything unusual. When a parameter moves and you don't know why, the journal shows what changed around that date.

Tap **Journal** in the row of buttons under the dashboard to open the **Tank Journal**. It's also in Settings → your tank → **Journal**.

![The Tank Journal in Cora Mobile](img/mobile-journal.webp "Entries newest first, with category chips to filter them.")

## Writing an entry {#adding-an-entry}

Tap **New Entry**. The form has:

- **TITLE** and **DESCRIPTION**, in plain words
- **CATEGORY**: Observation, Concern, Emergency, Change or Milestone. A concern or an emergency also gets a **SEVERITY** (Info, Warning or Critical)
- **DATE & TIME**, which starts at now. Change it if you're writing it up later
- **TAGS**, to find entries again
- **PHOTOS**, with **Take photo** or **Choose from library**. A coral colouring up, a pump acting strangely or a test kit result
- **Attach water params**, which saves the tank's current readings with the entry. It's what makes an entry useful months later

:::note Keep entries short
A short note is enough. "20 l water change, 5 ml extra alkalinity" says it all. Write it at the time if you can.
:::

## Dictating an entry {#dictating}

The round Cora button labelled **Journal** at the bottom right of the dashboard records a voice note, which is easier with wet hands. Tap it, say what happened (up to a minute), and tap again to stop. Cora turns what you said into one or more journal entries and tells you how many it saved. The same button is on the tank's timeline.

Dictation uses Cora Assistant. If you haven't allowed it to use your tank data yet, Cora asks first.

## What to record {#what-to-record}

These tend to matter most:

- Water changes: how much, and when
- Anything new in the tank: livestock, rock, media
- Dosing changes: what you changed and why
- Equipment: cleaned, replaced, moved, failed
- Anything unusual: a power cut, a hot day, a spill

Some entries are written for you. Completing a [maintenance](/help/mobile-maintenance) task adds one, and so does **Log the dose you gave** in the [dose calculator](/help/mobile-dosing#the-dose-calculator).

## Feeding {#feeding}

Tell Cora once how you usually feed, then log only the exceptions.

To set your routine, open **Edit tank** (the pencil at the top of the dashboard) and tap **Feeding routine**. A card on the dashboard, **How do you usually feed?**, takes you there with **Set up routine** until you've done it. For each food, give the food, an **Amount**, a **Unit** (cube, pinch, g, ml, drop, scoop or sheet) and **Times per day**. Tap **Add food** for more. Turn on **I use an auto feeder** if one feeds for you, and its feeds count as part of the routine. Under **Portion weights (optional)** you can say what one cube or pinch weighs, so Cora can compare feeds over time in grams.

When a day differs from the routine, tap **Feeding** in the row of buttons under the widgets, or the feeding icon at the top of the journal. The sheet **Log a feeding exception** has three choices: **Fed extra**, **Skipped a feed** and **Changed food or amount**. You can add an **Amount (optional)**. Each one is an ordinary journal entry, and Reef Buddy uses it, so a stable tank isn't blamed for a snack you gave it.

Other ways a feeding gets logged:

- On a Neptune Apex, a Feed A to D cycle is recorded when the Cora Max that reads your Apex sees it start. That covers a cycle you start at the Apex, in Apex Fusion or from the Apex's own schedule.
- Tell Cora Assistant, by voice or in a chat, something like *"Log that I fed mysis."* It writes the entry for you.

## Editing and removing entries {#editing}

Tap an entry to open and change it. To remove one, swipe it to the left and confirm. A deleted entry can't be brought back.

## Asking Cora about an entry {#analyze}

An entry in the **Concern** or **Emergency** category has an **Analyze** button. It sends the entry, with the tank's readings from around the same date, to [Cora Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant), and the assessment appears on the entry. Use it when you've written down something that worries you and want a second opinion.

## Reading it back {#reading-it-back}

Each tank has its own journal, newest first, with photos in line.

To narrow a long journal, tap a category chip at the top: **Observation**, **Concern**, **Emergency**, **Change** or **Milestone**.

The **Tank timeline** icon at the top opens the tank's story, where journal entries sit beside maintenance, livestock, water tests and alerts. See [Activity and timeline](/help/mobile-activity#tank-timeline).

Cora Assistant reads the journal too. It answers questions like *"when did I last change water?"* from your entries as well as your readings.

## On Cora Max {#on-cora-max}

You can add journal entries on Cora Max too, which is handy when you're standing at the tank. Open the journal from the tank menu, or dictate an entry.

The journal screen on Cora Max also has **Fed extra** and **Skipped a feed** buttons, with a line showing your feeding routine. If you haven't set one, the line says *Set your feeding routine in the phone app*, because you set the routine in Cora Mobile. More in [Journal, livestock and maintenance](/help/max-records) on Cora Max.
