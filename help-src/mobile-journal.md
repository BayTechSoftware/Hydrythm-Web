The journal is where you write down what you did to the tank: water changes, new livestock, dosing changes, equipment work and maintenance.

It pays off later. When a parameter moves and you don't know why, the journal shows what changed around that date.

![The journal](img/mobile-journal.webp "Entries in reverse order, with photos inline.")

## Adding an entry

Tap the **Journal** button floating at the bottom right of the Dashboard.

Write what you did in plain words. Add a photo if it helps, like a coral colouring up, a piece of equipment acting strangely or a test kit result.

You can also **dictate** an entry instead of typing, which is easier with wet hands. Speak, and Cora writes what you said into the entry.

Each entry gets the tank and the time added for you.

:::note Keep entries short
A short note is enough. "20 l water change, 5 ml extra alkalinity" says it all. Write it at the time. Entries pieced together later are less reliable.
:::

## What to record

These tend to matter most:

- Water changes: how much, and when
- Anything new in the tank: livestock, rock, media
- Dosing changes: what you changed and why
- Equipment: cleaned, replaced, moved, failed
- Maintenance: skimmer cleaned, socks changed, pumps serviced
- Anything unusual: a power cut, a hot day, a spill

## Editing and removing entries

Tap an entry to open and change it. To remove one, swipe it and confirm. Cora asks first, because you can't rebuild an entry you wrote at the time.

## Asking Cora about an entry

If you marked an entry as a **concern** or an **emergency**, it has an **Analyze** action. This sends the entry, with the tank's readings from around the same date, to [Cora Assistant](/help/mobile-assistant), and you get an assessment back. Use it when you've written down something that worries you and want a second opinion.

## Reading it back

Each tank has its own journal, newest first, with photos in line.

To narrow a long journal, filter by category: observations, concerns or emergencies.

The Assistant reads the journal. It answers questions like *"when did I last change water?"* from your entries as well as your readings.

## On Cora Max

You can add journal entries on Cora Max too, which is often handier when you're standing at the tank. Open the journal from the tank menu, or dictate an entry.
