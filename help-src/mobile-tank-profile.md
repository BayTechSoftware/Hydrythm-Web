The tank profile describes your system. Cora uses it to judge readings, work out doses and decide what's normal for your tank in particular.

To open it, tap the **pencil on the right of the tank header**. The small icon next to the tank name is a quick rename. It doesn't open the profile.

## Guided setup

There's a lot in a profile, so **Guided setup** takes you through it one section at a time: dosing, lighting, flow and filtration, livestock and your equipment.

You can stop and pick it up again whenever you like.

- **Finish later** takes you back and keeps the answers you've given so far.
- When you open guided setup again, it picks up at the section where you stopped.
- **Decide later** skips a question you're not ready to answer, and you can carry on with the rest.
- If you leave a section with unsaved changes, Cora asks first. You can pick **Keep editing** or **Leave**.

The completeness figure on the profile takes you back into the same flow. So does the prompt on the dashboard, which shows while the profile is below 100%.

:::note Each section changes what Cora can tell you
The more you fill in, the more Cora can do. Dosing products make the dose calculator and consumption tracking work. Livestock and tank type change what your readings are judged against. Equipment tells Cora what you have. With a thin profile, you'll get more general answers.
:::

## Completeness

The percentage at the top shows how much of the profile you've filled in. The fuller the profile, the more specific the advice. With an empty one, Cora falls back on defaults.

## Sections

| Section | Covers |
|---|---|
| Tank Name & Type | What you call the tank, its type, and its age |
| Tank Dimensions | Physical size and total water volume |
| Dosing | What you dose and how |
| Water Management | Water changes, top-off, salinity targets |
| Livestock | What the tank holds and how heavily it is stocked |
| Temperature Control | Heating and cooling equipment |
| Lighting | Fixtures and photoperiod |
| Flow & Filtration | Pumps, skimming and media |
| Neptune devices | The Apex on this tank and its modules |
| Red Sea devices | ReefBeat units assigned here |
| Cora Max | Which screens serve this tank |
| Parameter sources | Which source each parameter is read from |
| Pests & treatments | What you have dealt with, and what you used |

![The device and source sections](img/mobile-tank-profile-devices.webp "The lower half of the profile: your equipment, where each parameter comes from, and treatment history.")

Tap a section to open it. The info icon next to each one tells you what its fields are used for.

## Volume

Enter the real water volume, sump included. Don't use the display volume printed on the tank.

:::warning Doses scale directly with volume
If the volume is 20% too high, dose suggestions are 20% too high as well. If you're not sure, measure it. Don't guess.
:::

## Tank age

Set the date you started the tank. Cora judges readings against what's normal for a tank that age, so a three-month-old system and a five-year-old one are judged differently. If the tank is still cycling, say so.

## Keeping the profile current

Update the profile when your system changes, for example new equipment, different stocking or a new dosing routine. Cora works from the profile, so if it's out of date, the advice will be too.
