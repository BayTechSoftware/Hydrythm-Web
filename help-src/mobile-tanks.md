This page covers having more than one tank: adding one, moving between them, putting them in order, and removing one you no longer keep. Each tank has its own dashboard, devices, alerts and records.

You can have as many tanks as you like on any plan. Your plan only decides how many of them get Reef Buddy (see [The order decides which tanks get Reef Buddy](/help/mobile-tanks#reef-buddy-and-tank-order)).

## Adding another tank {#adding-another-tank}

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Add tank**, under your list of tanks.
2. **Add Tank** opens the full tank profile. Give the tank a name under **Tank Name & Type**, and fill in whatever else you know, like the type and the water volume under **Tank Dimensions**. You can finish the rest later. See [Your tank profile](/help/mobile-tank-profile).
3. Tap **Save Tank**. If this tank is brand new and still cycling, first turn on **This is a brand-new tank I'm cycling** at the bottom of the form.
4. Cora offers the first-test step. Log a test, set a weekly reminder, or tap **Later**. See [The first-test card](/help/mobile-setup#the-first-test-card).

Then give the tank its equipment:

- To add new equipment for it, go to **Devices → Add Device**, pick the brand and choose this tank. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections).
- To move equipment you already have, open the device on the **Devices** tab and change its tank. See [Adding, editing and removing devices](/help/mobile-devices).
- To show the tank on Cora Max, go to **Devices**, open your Cora Max and tap **Add Tank** under **Assigned Tanks**. A Cora Max shows up to 4 tanks.

:::tip Is the new tank still cycling?
You can also start guided cycling later: tap **Starting a new tank?** on the tank's dashboard (the card shows while the tank has no readings), or use the same card at the top of the tank's profile, which works at any time. More in [Guided journeys](/help/mobile-journeys).
:::

## Switching between tanks {#switching-between-tanks}

- **Dashboard**: swipe left or right. The dots at the top show which tank you're on.
- **Settings**: every tank has its own row. Tap one to open that tank's settings, like **Alert thresholds**, **Parameter Mapping** and **Units**.
- **Assistant** and **Intelligence**: pick the tank at the top of the screen. See [Asking Cora](/help/mobile-assistant#pick-a-tank-first).
- A notification about one tank opens that tank's dashboard.

On Cora Max, the Reef Room shows every tank side by side. See [The Reef Room](/help/max-reef-room).

## Changing the order {#changing-the-order}

Go to **Settings** and tap **Reorder tanks**, then drag the tanks into the order you want. You can also drag a tank by its handle in the list on the Settings screen. **Reorder tanks** only works once you have two tanks or more.

The order is the order you swipe through on the dashboard.

### The order decides which tanks get Reef Buddy {#reef-buddy-and-tank-order}

Your plan covers a set number of tanks for Reef Buddy's daily briefing. Cora gives it to the first ones in your **Reorder tanks** order. On Essential, and during the 30-day trial, that's your first tank only. Advanced covers 5 tanks and Expert covers 20. More in [Plans and subscriptions](/help/mobile-plans).

If a tank gets no briefing, move it to the top of the list. Removed tanks don't count.

## Removing a tank {#removing-a-tank}

1. Open the tank profile: tap the pencil in the tank header, or go to **Settings**, tap the tank and choose **Edit tank**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Remove Tank**.
3. Cora asks **Remove this tank?** Tap **Remove**.

Removing only hides the tank. It leaves your dashboard and your Settings list, and its readings, journal and records are kept. You can bring it back at any time.

A removed tank is kept for its history, but it is never shown and it stays silent. It sends no alerts and no reminders, it gets no Reef Buddy briefing and takes no place in your plan's Reef Buddy count, and its automations stop.

:::note A removed tank's equipment stays linked
Removing a tank doesn't unlink anything. Its devices stay assigned to it, and a Cora Max assigned to it stays assigned, but nothing from that tank is shown and no alert or reminder reaches you.

Before you remove a tank, move or remove its devices on the **Devices** tab. Then open your Cora Max under **Devices** and tap the **Remove tank** icon next to that tank under **Assigned Tanks**.
:::

When you remove a tank, a de-identified copy of its parameter history goes to reef research, with no link to you. If you don't want that, turn off **Contribute anonymized tank data** first. See [Your data](/help/mobile-data-export).

## Restoring a removed tank {#restoring-a-removed-tank}

Go to **Settings** and tap **Removed tanks**. The row only shows when you have a removed tank. Tap **Restore** next to the tank. Its readings, journal and devices come back exactly as they were.

Cora Mobile has no way to delete a single tank for good. The only way to delete a tank's data is to delete your account. See [Your data](/help/mobile-data-export).
