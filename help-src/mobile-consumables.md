Some equipment uses something up, like reagent, dosing liquid, top-off water or filter media. It can tell Cora how much is left, and Cora can warn you before it runs out.

## Setting a refill alert

Open the device from the **Devices** tab or by tapping its tile on the dashboard. Then tap the **bell** in the top bar.

![Refill alerts for a dosing unit](img/mobile-consumables.webp "One threshold per head, each switched on or off independently.")

If the equipment has more than one container, each container gets its own threshold. A head you watch closely and one you rarely touch can be set differently.

Most thresholds are in days, not volume. Cora works out how long what's left will last at the rate you're using it. "Four days of calcium left" is something you can act on. "180 mL left" isn't.

| Device | Threshold on |
|---|---|
| Trident | Tests remaining, and how full the waste bottle is |
| Dosing head | Days of supplement left. Some also offer millilitres remaining |
| ATO | Days of reservoir left |
| Mat roller | Days of roll left |

A consumable alert works like any other alert. It shows in the [Alert Center](/help/mobile-alerts) and can push to your phone. You get **one** notification when a level is crossed, and it clears when the level is back above the threshold.

## Choosing a threshold

Set it early enough that you have time to act. If it fires the day something runs out, it's no warning at all.

:::warning Some equipment keeps going when it's empty
A dosing head with an empty container keeps running its schedule and reports doses it never delivered. A refill alert is how you catch this, so set one for every head you dose from.
:::

## After refilling

Reset or update the level on the device's page. The alert clears and the next warning is worked out correctly.
