Livestock is your list of the corals, fish and invertebrates in your tank. For each one, it keeps the date you added it and how it's doing now. Open it from the shortcut row at the bottom of the dashboard.

![The livestock inventory](img/mobile-livestock.webp "Inventory summary and recent losses, with entries grouped by type.")

## The summary

At the top you'll see two things.

- **Inventory Summary** gives totals for broad groups like corals, fish and inverts.
- **Losses** counts the entries you've marked lost in the last 90 days.

Below that, entries are grouped by type (SPS coral, LPS coral, soft coral, fish and so on). Each one shows its quantity and the date you added it.

Tap **Show Lost** in the top bar to include things that are no longer in the tank.

## Adding something

Each entry has these fields.

- **Name** is whatever you call it.
- **Type** is SPS coral, LPS coral, soft coral, fish, invertebrate, anemone or other.
- **Species** is optional. Fill it in if you know it.
- **Quantity** is for groups, like a shoal or a frag pack.
- **Date added** starts on today.
- **Notes** holds anything else you want to keep, such as supplier, placement or cost.

## What happened to it

Every entry has a status.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Alive** | Still in the tank |
| **Lost** | Died |
| **Fragged** | Cut up, and the colony lives on |
| **Sold** | Went to someone else |
| **Moved** | In another of your tanks |

When you mark something **lost**, Cora saves the date. You can add a reason too. If you log every loss, patterns show up later. Maybe the losses cluster in one corner of the tank, or they follow a particular event.

:::note "Unknown" is a fine reason
Log the date even when you don't know the cause. The date is what you'll compare against later.
:::

## Livestock and the rest of Cora

Cora judges your parameters partly by how stocked the tank is. A packed SPS system and a lightly stocked softie tank aren't held to the same standard.

You can also ask about it. Cora answers questions like *"When did I add the hammer coral?"* or *"What have I lost this year?"* from this list.
