Livestock is your list of the corals, fish and invertebrates in your tank. For each one, it keeps the date you added it and what has happened to it since.

Tap **Livestock** in the row of buttons under the dashboard. It's also in Settings → your tank → **Livestock**.

![The livestock inventory](img/mobile-livestock.webp "Inventory summary and recent losses, with entries grouped by type.")

## The summary {#summary}

At the top you'll see the **Inventory Summary**, with totals for corals, fish and inverts, and how many losses you've recorded in the last 90 days.

Below that, entries are grouped by type, such as SPS Coral, LPS Coral, Soft Coral and Fish. Each one shows its quantity and the date you added it.

Tap **Show Lost** at the top to see what's no longer in the tank. **Show Alive** takes you back.

## Adding something {#adding-something}

Tap **Add Livestock** at the bottom of the screen and fill in:

- **Name \***, whatever you call it. It's the only field you must fill in.
- **Type**: SPS Coral, LPS Coral, Soft Coral, Fish, Invertebrate, Anemone or Other.
- **Species (optional)**, if you know it.
- **Quantity**, for groups like a shoal or a frag pack.
- **Notes (optional)**, for anything else, such as supplier, placement or cost.

The date added is set to today when you save.

## When something changes {#status}

Tap an entry to see what you can do with it:

- **Mark as Lost** when something dies. If the entry has a quantity above one, Cora asks **How many lost?**, so you can record part of a group. Set the date it was lost and pick a **Reason**, such as bleaching, RTN, aggression or unknown.
- **Fragged** when you've cut it up and the colony lives on.
- **Sold** when it went to someone else.
- **Moved** when it's now in another of your tanks.
- On an entry that's no longer alive, **Restore to alive** undoes the change, for example if you marked it by mistake.
- **Edit** changes the details. **Delete** removes the entry altogether.
- **View history** opens the item's story: when it was added, and when it was lost or left the tank.

If you log every loss, patterns show up later. Maybe the losses cluster in one corner of the tank, or they follow a particular event.

:::note "unknown" is a fine reason
Log the date even when you don't know the cause. The date is what you'll compare against later.
:::

## Livestock and the rest of Cora {#livestock-and-cora}

Cora judges your parameters partly by how stocked the tank is. A packed SPS system and a lightly stocked softie tank aren't held to the same standard.

You can also ask about it. Cora Assistant answers questions like *"When did I add the hammer coral?"* or *"What have I lost this year?"* from this list.

Changes to your livestock also show up on the [tank timeline](/help/mobile-activity#tank-timeline). Write the story behind a loss or a new arrival in the [journal](/help/mobile-journal).
