## Quick start

1. Install Cora Mobile, sign up and add your first tank. See [Setting up Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup).
2. Connect your equipment from **Devices → Add Device**, or log a water test by hand. See [Connecting your equipment](/help/mobile-connections).
3. Check the alert range for each parameter. See [Alerts and thresholds](/help/mobile-alerts).
4. Let Cora Mobile send notifications, and pick which ones. See [Notifications](/help/mobile-notifications).
5. If you have a Cora Max, pair it from your phone. See [Setting up Cora Max](/help/max-setup).

:::tip Start on your phone
Even if you bought Cora Max, [set up your tanks and devices in Cora Mobile](/help/mobile-setup) first. Typing is easier on a phone, and Cora Max picks everything up from your account.
:::

## Cora Mobile and Cora Max

**Cora Mobile** is the phone app. You set almost everything up here (tanks, devices, dashboards, alerts, dosing products), and it's what you carry when you're away from the tank. The icon on your home screen says **Cora**.

**Cora Max** is the wall screen for the reef room. It shows the same tanks and the same live data, big enough to read from across the room, reads your equipment around the clock, and you can talk to it.

Both use the same account, so your tanks, readings, devices and records are the same on each. Each screen keeps its own dashboard layout, and a Cora Max's Wi-Fi, brightness, sound, wake word and child lock apply only to that screen. More in [More than one Cora device](/help/mobile-multi-device).

Every reading shows its source and age. If two sources disagree, such as a probe and a test kit, Cora tells you instead of quietly averaging them. When something starts to drift, **Reef Buddy** brings it up in your daily briefing.
