## The two halves of Cora

**Cora Mobile** is the phone app. It is where you set almost everything up (tanks, devices, dashboards, alerts, dosing products), and it is what you carry when you are away from the tank. On your home screen the icon is simply **Cora**.

**Cora Max** is the wall-mounted command centre for the reef room. It shows the same tanks and the same live data on a screen you can read from across the room, and it takes voice.

They share one account, so your tanks, readings, devices and records are the same on both, and you do not set them up twice.

Some things stay with the screen they belong to. Each screen keeps its **own dashboard layout**, and settings that describe a particular screen (Wi-Fi, brightness, audio, wake word, child lock) belong to that screen alone. See [What is shared and what is not](/help/mobile-multi-device).

:::tip Start on your phone
Even if Cora Max is what you bought, set your tanks and devices up in Cora Mobile first. Everything is easier to type on a phone, and Cora Max picks it all up automatically.
:::

## What Cora adds to a controller

Cora shows you a number together with what it depends on: where it came from, and how old it is.

Every reading carries where it came from and how old it is. When two sources disagree (a probe against a test kit, a doser against your ICP results), Cora says so rather than quietly averaging them. And when something drifts, **Reef Buddy** tells you in the morning instead of waiting for you to notice.
