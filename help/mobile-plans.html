Plans and subscriptions

Monitoring your tank is free, with no limit. The paid plans give you bigger AI allowances and add Reef Buddy. This page covers what each plan includes and how to subscribe, change or cancel.

What is always free

  • Live readings from your equipment, with dashboards and charts
  • Devices, alerts and thresholds
  • Journal, maintenance and livestock
  • Push notifications for critical alerts
  • Guided cycling for a new tank
  • As many tanks as you like. AI features cover your first tank (see Which tanks get AI)
  • Exporting your data

When an allowance runs out, you can still read everything Cora has already made for you.

The trial

Your 30-day Advanced trial starts when you create your account. It doesn't wait until you first use a feature, and you don't need a card. Account & Subscription shows it as Cora Advanced Trial, with the date it ends.

During the trial
Assistant messages100 a month
Voice sessions60 a month
Health reports4 a month
ICP analyses1 a month
Reef Buddy and AI monitoringYour first tank

The trial includes Reef Buddy and Design with AI. Its allowances are smaller than the Advanced plan's.

When it ends, your account goes back to Free. Nothing is deleted. You can still read every reading, journal entry, report and briefing you have.

Paid tiers

There are three plans, Essential, Advanced and Expert, each monthly or annual. Settings shows them as Cora Essential, Cora Advanced and Cora Expert. You'll find current prices in Cora Mobile and in your app store, since that's where you pay.

Voice sessions have their own count, and every spoken reply also uses up your message allowance. Every paid plan includes Reef Buddy, ICP upload and analysis for the labs Cora reads, comparison with your own history, and Design with AI for dashboards (up to 5 designs a day for each tank).

The Expert plan isn't the Cora Max screen

The Expert plan is a subscription. Cora Max is the screen that sits by your tank. You don't need one to have the other.

Which tanks get AI

Reef Buddy and AI monitoring cover as many tanks as your plan allows, counted from the top of Settings → Reorder tanks. To bring a tank in, move it up the list. Removed tanks don't take a place.

Subscribing or upgrading

  1. Open Settings and tap the account card at the top. Account & Subscription opens.
  2. Next to your plan, tap See plans (on Free or the trial), Upgrade (on Essential) or Upgrade to Expert (on Advanced).
  3. Choose Monthly or Annual. The annual price shows how much you save.
  4. Pick a plan and tap Subscribe.
  5. Confirm with the App Store or Google Play. Your new plan unlocks as soon as the store confirms it.

The same plan picker also opens when you reach a limit, for example You've hit your message limit, and from the locked Reef Buddy card.

Seeing what you have used

Settings → Cora Assistant → Usage & Limits shows each metered feature next to its monthly allowance, all on one screen. That's Assistant messages, ICP analyses and health reports.

You can also see usage where you use each feature.

  • In Assistant, this month's messages are shown above the message box.
  • In Intelligence, this month's ICP analyses and health reports are shown under the buttons.

The counts start again at the beginning of each calendar month.

Changing plan

To move up, use Upgrade or Upgrade to Expert as above. To move down or switch between monthly and annual, use Manage Subscription. Changes go through the App Store or Google Play and follow the same rules as any other subscription on your phone, including that store's refund and cancellation terms.

Cancelling

You cancel with the store you paid. You can't cancel inside Cora. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then goes back to Free.

The quickest way is Settings → your account card → Manage Subscription. If you bought the plan on the other kind of phone, Cora says so (Bought on the App Store or Bought on Google Play) and Continue opens that store's page.

If Manage Subscription isn't there, go to the store directly.

  • iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Pick Cora and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Pick Cora and tap Cancel subscription.

Cancelling doesn't delete anything

Your tanks, readings, history and journal stay as they are. You lose the paid features, but you keep your data. Come back later and it's all still there.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel

If you plan to delete your account, cancel the subscription first. Deleting the account doesn't stop the store billing you. See Deleting your account.

If your plan is not showing

Sometimes a purchase takes a moment to reach Cora Mobile. If it's been a few minutes, try these.

  1. Close Cora completely and open it again.
  2. Make sure you're signed in to the same account that made the purchase.
  3. Make sure the store account on your phone is the one you bought with.

If it still isn't there, tap Restore Purchases. If Settings says Couldn't verify your plan, check your connection and tap Retry. Your access doesn't change while Cora checks. If none of that works, email cora@coraiq.tech with the date you bought it.

Your data stays yours whatever plan you're on. More in Exporting your data and deleting your account. To report a billing problem from inside Cora Mobile, see Reporting a problem.
