Signing in and account recovery

On this page

  • Changing your password or email later

  • Signing out

Your account keeps your tanks, readings and devices together. This page helps you get into it and keep it in order. Creating an account for the first time is covered in Setting up Cora Mobile.

Can't sign in?

Start with the one that matches what you see.

  • You forgot your password. See Forgotten password.
  • Cora says the address already has an account with a different sign-in method. You first signed up another way. Sign in the way you did the first time: with your email and password, with Google, or with Apple.
  • You're in, but your tanks are missing. You're probably in a second, empty account. That happens when the other sign-in method uses a different address, such as Apple's Hide My Email or another Google account. Your tanks aren't gone. Sign out and sign in the way you did the first time.
  • Cora says there were too many sign-in attempts. Wait a few minutes, or tap Forgot password? to reset your password and sign in right away.
  • Cora says the account has been disabled. The message names an address to write to. Email it from the address you signed up with to have the account restored.
  • The sign-in won't go ahead at all. Tick I agree to: Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy first. Every way of signing in needs it.

Ways to sign in

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. Use the same method every time.

If you use Continue with Apple, choose Share My Email when Apple asks, so lab and ICP results can reach your Cora account. If you already chose to hide it, add your real address under Lab and ICP emails.

Forgotten password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email address and tap Send Link. Open the link on the same device and set a new password.

The message is the same either way

You'll see "if that address has a Cora account, a reset link is on its way", whether or not the address is registered. This stops anyone using the screen to find out who has an account. If no email arrives, you probably signed up with a different address.

If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder. Then try the other ways to sign in. An account created with Continue with Apple or Continue with Google has no Cora password to reset.

A new password needs at least 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol.

Verifying your email

After you sign up with an email address, Cora sends a verification email from noreply@coraiq.tech with the subject "Confirm your email for Cora". Tap Verify my email in it. The link expires after 24 hours. Then go back to Cora Mobile and tap I've Verified My Email.

If the email hasn't arrived, check your spam folder, then tap Resend verification email on the verification screen. Cora sends at most one verification email a minute and five an hour, and the first one counts. If you tap too soon, Cora asks you to wait.

Still verifying

Cora Mobile checks your account when you tap I've Verified My Email. If your email still isn't verified, tap Verify my email in the newest message, wait a moment and check again. If the link has expired, tap Resend verification email. Once verification goes through, it applies on all your devices.

The email address is wrong

If you mistyped it, you don't have to start again. Tap Wrong address? Change email on the verification screen and enter the right address. Cora sends a new link to it. After you tap that link, sign in again with the new address.

To start over with a different account, tap Sign out on the verification screen.

"Sign out and sign in again, then retry"

Changing an account's email address is a security step, so Cora sometimes wants a recent sign-in first. If it asks you to sign out and back in, do that, then tap Wrong address? Change email again.

Changing your password or email later

Cora Mobile has no setting for either one once your email is verified.

  • For your password, sign out, then use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
  • For your email address, contact support from Settings → Help & Support. See Reporting a problem.
  • An Apple or Google account's password and address are managed by Apple or Google.

Your name

Go to Settings, then the account card at the top, and tap Display name. Cora Assistant uses it to address you.

Lab and ICP emails

Labs and emailed ICP results find your account by email address. If your lab knows a different address from the one you sign in with, or you signed in with a hidden Apple email, add it under Settings → your account card → Lab & ICP emails. Tap Add lab email, then Send code, enter the 6-digit code from the email and tap Verify. You can add up to 3. The steps are in ICP and health reports.

Signing out

Settings → your account → Sign Out signs this device out. Nothing is deleted, and when you sign back in your tanks, readings and devices are all there. Your data explains how signing out differs from deleting your account.
