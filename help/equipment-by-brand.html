Equipment by brand

On this page

  • Neptune Apex, DŌS and Trident
  • Red Sea ReefBeat
  • Jecod and Jebao
  • AquaWiz KH Controller
  • Anything else

Find your brand to see every page about it: how to add it, how to control it on your phone and on Cora Max, which alerts it raises, and what to do when it misbehaves.

  • Neptune™ Apex, DŌS and Trident
  • Red Sea™ ReefBeat
  • Jecod™ and Jebao™
  • AquaWiz™ KH Controller

You add every brand on your phone, from Devices → Add Device. Neptune Apex, Red Sea and Jecod are read on your home network: without a Cora Max, your phone reads them only while Cora Mobile is open on the same Wi-Fi. More in Connecting your equipment.

Neptune Apex, DŌS and Trident

Cora reads your Apex's probes and outlets, runs its feed cycles, and works with its DŌS doser and Trident tester.

  • Add it: Connecting your equipment
  • Tell Cora what each probe measures: Parameter Mapping and probes
  • Control it on your phone: Apex, DŌS, Trident
  • On Cora Max: outlets and feed mode, Apex and Trident, DŌS heads
  • Alerts: stopped reporting, doser alerts
  • Fixes: "Could not reach the Apex", "… has no Apex details", "Apex password", Feed doesn't start, a dose stopped partway

Red Sea ReefBeat

ReefDose, ReefRun, ReefMat and ReefATO+. A device's own settings only open when your phone is on the same network as the device.

  • Add it: Connecting your equipment
  • Control it on your phone: Red Sea ReefBeat, ReefDose, ReefRun, ReefMat, ReefATO+
  • On Cora Max: Red Sea ReefBeat
  • Alerts: stopped reporting, doser alerts
  • Fixes: "Red Sea readings have stopped updating", settings greyed out

Jecod and Jebao

Pumps that reach Cora over your network or over Bluetooth. Which one decides what you can do from away.

  • Add it: Connecting your equipment
  • Control it on your phone: Jecod pumps, schedules
  • On Cora Max: Jecod
  • Alerts: stopped reporting
  • Fixes: "Could not reach this pump", out of Bluetooth range

AquaWiz KH Controller

Cora reads it through AquaWiz's own cloud every 12 hours, and whenever you open its page, so it keeps updating without a Cora Max. You sign in once to link it and Cora keeps only your username. Pump and pH probe alerts work for every AquaWiz; a Cora Max at the tank reads it every hour instead and adds water box alerts. See How Cora reads your AquaWiz.

  • Add it: Connecting your equipment
  • On your phone: AquaWiz
  • On Cora Max: AquaWiz
  • Alerts: stopped reporting
  • Fixes: a reading is stale, sign-in refused

Anything else

For equipment Cora doesn't connect to, log readings by hand. See Logging by hand.
