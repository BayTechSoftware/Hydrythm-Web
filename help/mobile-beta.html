Beta features

On this page

  • Who gets Beta
  • The Beta integrations
  • Labelled Beta, open to everyone
  • What Beta means day to day

Some of Cora's newest integrations are in Beta. We're still testing them, so some readings or controls may be limited and what you see can change between updates.

Beta

Beta. The Beta integrations are only open to Beta users. If you are not a Beta user you will not see them in Add device. To ask for Beta access, contact support.

Who gets Beta

Beta access belongs to your Cora account. It's switched on by us, for an account, and you can't turn it on yourself in Settings. Being in a store testing programme doesn't switch it on either. To ask, write to support from the email on your account.

Once your account is a Beta user, the Beta rows show up in Add device on Cora Mobile. You don't need to restart the app.

If you're not a Beta user, Add device lists Cora, Neptune™ Apex, Red Sea™, Jecod™ and AquaWiz™. Equipment you've already linked stays on your Devices tab.

The Beta integrations

Each one is added from Devices → Add Device. A row marked Requires Cora Max is greyed out until the tank has a Cora Max, because that Cora Max does the reading.

Maxspect™Gyre pumps: wave pattern and speed. See Connecting your equipment.Yes
GHL™ProfiLux and Mitras controllers: probes, sockets, dosers and level sensors. See Connecting your equipment.Yes
HYDROS™Readings and, with a write key, control, through the HYDROS cloud. See Connecting your equipment.No
Focustronic™Alkatronic, Dosetronic and Mastertronic, through your Focustronic account. See Connecting your equipment.No
EcoTech™Radion lights, VorTech pumps and Versa dosers. See EcoTech lights, pumps and dosers.Yes
Inkbird™IAM-T1 and IAM-T2 room air monitors. See Connecting your equipment.Yes
CameraWyze™, Tapo™, Sonoff™ and other RTSP cameras, plus Skimmer Watch. See Cameras and Skimmer Watch.Yes

Labelled Beta, open to everyone

A few features carry (Beta) in their name but don't need Beta access. Everyone sees them.

  • Large doses (Beta) on a DŌS head, described in Controlling your equipment
  • Dosing plan (Beta) on a ReefDose
  • Pump schedule (Beta) on a ReefRun
  • ReefMat settings (Beta) and New roll (Beta) on a ReefMat

What Beta means day to day

  • A Beta page may not match this guide the week after an update. If something doesn't work as described, tell us from Getting help.
  • Some commands go through the maker's own servers or app, which Cora doesn't control. The page says so where it matters.
  • Equipment we haven't been able to test on real hardware is marked as such.

Cora Max shows the Beta devices your account has linked. See Devices and device health.
