Activity and timeline

On this page

  • What is recorded
  • What caused it
  • The tank timeline

Activity is the answer to "something changed and I don't know why."

Settings → Activity.

What is recorded

Every action that reached your equipment:

  • Outlet switches
  • Feed cycles
  • Doses
  • Smart plug changes
  • Anything a scene or automation did

What caused it

This is the column that matters. Each entry names its cause:

CauseMeans
YouSomeone tapped it, on the phone or on Cora Max
A ruleAn automation fired
A scheduleA timed action
The AssistantYou asked Cora to do it
The equipmentThe device did it on its own programming

When a pump turns off unexpectedly, this is the first place to look, and usually the last — either a rule you forgot about did it, or it wasn't Cora at all.

The tank timeline

Separately from equipment actions, each tank has a timeline — readings, alerts, journal entries, ICP results and livestock changes laid out in order.

Use activity when you are asking "what did something do?" and the timeline when you are asking "what was happening around this date?"

Pair the timeline with the journal

The timeline shows what Cora recorded. The journal shows what you did. Read together they usually explain each other — a parameter that moved the day after a water change is not a mystery.

Written for Cora Max 0.28.28 and Cora Mobile 0.5.52.
