Maintenance

On Cora Max: Journal, livestock and maintenance

On this page

  • The summary
  • Adding a task
  • Reminders
  • Marking a task done
  • Marking a task done earlier
  • Editing a task
  • Snoozing

Maintenance keeps the jobs your tank needs on a regular basis, with a reminder for each one.

Tap Maintenance in the row of buttons under the dashboard. It's also in Settings → your tank → Maintenance.

The summary

At the top you'll see Maintenance Compliance, the share of tasks that are on schedule right now, and how many tasks are overdue.

Each task in the list is colour-coded as overdue, due soon or scheduled. It also shows when you last did it and when it's due next.

Adding a task

Tap Add Task, then choose:

  • From Template for a common job with its interval filled in. Pick it, then set when it's next due and the time you want to be reminded, and tap Confirm.
  • Custom Task for your own. Give it a Task name, an Interval (days), a Category and a Next due date. Turn on Reminder and pick Remind me at if you want a notification.

Some of the templates, with the interval they start on:

You can change the interval of any task later.

They work like any task you add yourself. You can rename them, change the interval, snooze them or delete them. If you delete one, Cora won't add it back for that device.

Test water

If you tap Remind me to test every week on the first-test card, Cora adds a weekly task called Test water, due on the Test day you pick. It works like any other task. See Setting up Cora Mobile. You can also add a testing reminder at any time with the Test water parameters template.

From an automation

An automation with the action Create a Maintenance Task can put a job on this list when its rule fires, or make an existing task due now. A new one is a One-off task, due now, and it shows Raised by an automation with the reason. It leaves the list when you complete it. While it's still open, the same automation doesn't add another. You get one notification.

Reminders

Open a task and turn on Reminder, then pick Remind me at. You get a notification the day before it's due and one on the day. While it's overdue, you get a reminder every few days. If several tasks on a tank come due together, they arrive as one notification.

Reminders come under Maintenance Reminders in Settings → Notifications. If you'd rather just see a task in the list, turn its Reminder off.

Marking a task done

Swipe the task to the right, or open it and tap Complete. If you swiped by mistake, tap Undo on the message that appears.

Cora saves the time you finished and counts the next due date from then. So if you do a job three days late, the next one moves out by three days too. If the task is overdue, Cora first asks When did you do it? Pick Just now, or Pick date and time if you did it earlier (see below).

The task's sheet shows its Completion history, including how late each one was.

Completing a task also writes a journal entry

Cora adds a short entry to your journal for you, noting if it was late. Add your own entry when there's more to say, for example when the pump was full of sand or the socks were black after one day.

Marking a task done earlier

Did the job yesterday and forgot to tick it off? Open the task and tap Done earlier…, then pick the date and the time. Cora dates the completion, the journal entry and the next due date from that time, so the next due date is the time you picked plus the interval.

You can go back up to a year, but not before the last time you completed the task. If you pick a time that hasn't happened yet, or one before the last completion, Cora says so and saves nothing.

Editing a task

Tap a task to open it. You can change the Task name, Interval (days), Notes (optional), Next due date and the reminder, then tap Save. Delete removes it.

Snoozing

Can't get to a job right now? Open it, tap Snooze and pick 1 Day, 3 Days, 1 Week, 2 Weeks or 1 Month. It stays in the list as Snoozed with its new date, and you won't be reminded until then.

Only mark a task done when you've done it

The next due date is counted from the time you did the task. If you mark an open task as done, every later due date shifts. If you did it earlier, use Done earlier… and pick the real time. If you haven't done it, snooze it instead.

There's no way to pause a task. For a job that doesn't apply this season, snooze it for 1 Month, or delete it and add it again later.
