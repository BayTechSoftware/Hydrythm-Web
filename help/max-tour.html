The Cora Max home screen

On Cora Mobile: Finding your way around Cora Mobile

On this page

  • The top bar
  • The dashboard
  • The tank menu
  • Switching tanks
  • The Outlets & Feed drawer
  • If something looks out of place

This page explains what you see on Cora Max. It shows one tank at a time, with live readings filling the screen so you can read them from across the room.

The top bar

From left to right:

  1. The grid icon opens the Reef Room, the overview of every tank this screen shows.
  2. The tank name, with a small arrow. Tap it to open the tank menu, which has every screen for the tank you're looking at. The full list is below.
  3. Alert tags show what needs attention right now: readings out of range, device faults, missed doses or low supplies. Tap a tag to bring that alert back to the middle of the screen. If there are more than fit, you'll see +2 (or however many). Tap it to see every active alarm.
  4. The clock. You can hide it with Show clock in Settings → Cora Max Settings.
  5. The status tag shows what this screen is doing right now. Green means all is well, blue means busy, amber needs a look, and red is a fault. Every status is listed below.
  6. Battery and Wi-Fi.
  7. The devices icon opens Devices, filtered to this tank. A tank with no devices doesn't show it.
  8. The Reef Buddy icon opens today's briefing. A dot means you haven't read it yet.
  9. The Cora Assistant icon starts a voice conversation.
  10. The gear opens settings.

Cora Assistant starts listening right away

Tapping the Cora Assistant icon starts a live voice session. If you wanted settings, that's the gear on the far right.

What the status tag means

TagMeaning
OnlineThis screen is collecting your readings, and they're up to date
CloudAnother Cora is collecting this tank's readings and this screen is showing them. They're just as up to date as with Online. If you have more than one Cora, the one that isn't collecting shows this
Polling Apex, Voice activeBusy with something right now (blue)
Polling offCollecting is turned off for this tank. You can turn it back on in Cora Mobile
UpdatingCollecting is paused while an update installs
StaleReadings have stopped coming in. The screen shows the last ones it got
Apex retry 12sYour Apex didn't answer. Cora Max tries again when the countdown ends
Cloud sync failedYour Apex answered, but its readings couldn't be saved to Cora Cloud, so the dashboard falls behind. Cora Max keeps trying
Cloud save refusedCora Cloud is refusing to save some of this screen's data. Nothing is wrong with your tank or with Cora Max, and nothing needs changing on this screen. The fix is a Cora Cloud update. Open Settings → Cora Max Settings and scroll to the Status section, where the Cloud save line says which data is affected. Then contact support
OfflineNo connection. The screen shows the last data it got
Offline, retrying in 30sYour network is up, but Cora Cloud has been out of reach for about 15 seconds. Cora Max reconnects by itself, and the countdown shows when it tries next
Main Cora offlineThis screen is a second Cora Max for this tank, and the Primary Cora Max (the one pinned to read this tank's equipment) has gone offline. This screen keeps showing the last data it has until the primary comes back or you choose a different Primary Cora Max. More in More than one Cora device

How the retry countdown works

Cora Max tries to reconnect at a steady pace. It waits about 15 seconds after the first drop, then 15 seconds again, then 30 seconds twice, then once a minute until it gets through. It never gives up. Offline, retrying in 30s is normal.

The dashboard

The rest of the screen is the dashboard, a fixed grid that keeps every widget visible. It doesn't scroll.

Widgets work the same as on your phone, but big enough to read from a few steps back. What each shape shows is in the Widget reference. To change what's on the dashboard, see Editing the Cora Max dashboard.

Every widget with a measured parameter shows its age and source, just like on your phone. A number with 2d next to it is two days old. Device and control tiles show their own state instead.

When the screen is dimmed

After the Dim after time with no touch, the screen dims. Your first tap only wakes it, so it can't press a control by accident. Tap again to use the screen.

Switching tanks

Tap the grid icon at the far left of the top bar to go to the Reef Room, then open the tank you want. You can also swipe sideways on the dashboard. Each tank has its own dashboard layout, so the whole screen changes as you switch.

The Outlets & Feed drawer

On a tank with a Neptune™ Apex, a small Outlets & Feed label sits at the bottom of the dashboard. Tap it, or swipe up anywhere on the dashboard, to open Controls.

  • FEED starts one of your Apex feed cycles with A, B, C or D. Cancel ends it early.
  • Outlets can each be switched between Auto, Off and On.

It lists the outlets and feed cycles you picked for this dashboard, or all of them if you haven't picked any. More in Outlets and controls.

This drawer controls real equipment

A command goes out the moment you tap, but sending it doesn't mean it's done. It comes back Confirmed, Unconfirmed, Refused or No change, and you can see which in Activity. Feed mode is the safe way to pause flow for feeding, because it puts everything back by itself. A manual Off stays off until you change it back.

If something looks out of place

If readings look stale, or the status tag is amber or red, start with Troubleshooting.
