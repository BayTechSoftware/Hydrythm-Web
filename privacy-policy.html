Privacy Policy

Cora IQ ("we", "us", "our") operates the Cora mobile application and related cloud services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password (email/password sign-in)
  • Name and email address from your Google account (Google Sign-In)
  • Name and email address from your Apple ID (Sign in with Apple)
  • Email verification status

Account credentials are managed securely through Cora Cloud. We do not store your raw password; credentials are hashed and stored using industry-standard practices.

1.2 Tank Profile Data

When you create a tank profile, we store the following in Cora Cloud:

  • Tank name, volume, type, salinity target, and other configuration details
  • Selected dashboard metrics and display preferences
  • Associated device IDs and integration settings

1.3 Water Parameter Data

We store water chemistry parameters (pH, alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate, phosphate, salinity, temperature, ORP, and others) that you enter manually, import from a Neptune Apex controller, or receive from Cora devices. All parameter data is stored in Cora Cloud under your user account.

1.4 ICP Report Data

When you upload an ICP (Inductively Coupled Plasma) water analysis report:

  • The PDF file is stored in Cora Cloud under your user account
  • The PDF content is processed by the Cora Assistant to extract and analyze parameters
  • The extracted parameter values and AI-generated analysis are stored in Cora Cloud

1.5 Health Reports

When you generate a health report:

  • Your tank parameters, livestock inventory, and journal entries are processed by the Cora Assistant
  • The AI-generated health assessment is stored in Cora Cloud under your user account

1.6 Cora Assistant (Text)

When you use the text-based Cora Assistant:

  • Your messages and the AI responses are stored in Cora Cloud under your user account
  • Your messages, along with your tank context (parameters, livestock, journal entries), are processed by the Cora Assistant to generate responses
  • A daily message limit applies
  • Conversation history is retained in Cora Cloud until you delete your account

1.7 Cora Assistant (Voice)

When you use the voice-based Cora Assistant:

  • Audio from your device microphone is transmitted in real time over a secure connection for processing by the Cora Assistant
  • The assistant processes the audio to generate spoken responses streamed back to your device
  • Voice sessions are ephemeral; audio is not stored on our servers or in your account
  • Text transcripts of voice conversations may be stored in Cora Cloud under your user account

1.8 Livestock Tracking

When you track livestock (coral, fish, invertebrates), the species, name, date added, and notes are stored in Cora Cloud under your user account.

1.9 Journal Entries

When you create journal entries:

  • Entry text, date, tags, and associated parameters are stored in Cora Cloud
  • Journal data may be processed by the Cora Assistant when generating AI-powered correlations and health reports

1.10 Maintenance Scheduler

When you create maintenance tasks, the task name, schedule, and completion history are stored in Cora Cloud. Push notifications for due tasks are delivered through Cora Cloud.

1.11 Parameter Alerts

When you configure parameter alerts, your alert thresholds and notification preferences are stored in Cora Cloud. Push notifications for triggered alerts are delivered through Cora Cloud.

1.12 Push Notifications

  • We use Cora Cloud to deliver push notifications for maintenance reminders and parameter alerts
  • Your device notification token is stored in Cora Cloud to route notifications to your device
  • You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or within the app

1.13 Neptune Apex Integration

When you connect a Neptune Apex controller:

  • Your Apex IP address and credentials are stored locally on your device only, using encrypted storage
  • The app communicates with your Apex controller over your local network; Apex data does not pass through our servers
  • Parameter readings retrieved from the Apex are stored in Cora Cloud as part of your tank data
Cora's integrations are independent: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any equipment manufacturer. Product names, logos and images are property of their respective owners. Some devices are connected through APIs that are not officially supported by their manufacturers; support will be updated as official APIs arrive.

1.14 AquaWiz Integration

When you link an AquaWiz KH Controller, Cora signs in to AquaWiz's online service on your behalf and reads that controller's measurements. Unlike the Neptune Apex integration, this one is not local; it runs in Cora Cloud, so your AquaWiz sign-in is held on our servers rather than only on your phone:

  • Your AquaWiz username, and an access token issued to you by AquaWiz, are stored in Cora Cloud. The token is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM under a key held in a dedicated secrets manager, separate from the database
  • Your AquaWiz password is stored only if you choose to save it when linking, and it is encrypted the same way. If you choose not to save it, we keep only the access token, and you will be asked to reconnect when it expires, roughly every 30 days
  • Cora connects to AquaWiz's servers on your behalf about every 30 minutes to retrieve alkalinity readings, and pH readings if you enable that option. Those readings are stored in Cora Cloud as part of your tank data
  • AquaWiz issues a single access token per account, so the token Cora holds is the same one their own app uses. We cannot revoke Cora's access independently. To cut it off, unlink the controller in Cora, or change your AquaWiz password
  • Your stored AquaWiz sign-in is permanently deleted when you unlink the controller, and when you delete your Cora account

1.15 Cora Devices

When you pair a Cora hardware device:

  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) is used during initial device provisioning
  • After provisioning, the app communicates with devices over your local network
  • Device telemetry (sensor readings, operational status) is stored in Cora Cloud under your user account

1.16 Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using encrypted storage:

  • Display and theme preferences
  • Neptune Apex credentials and IP address
  • Device connection history and cached data
  • App configuration and preferences

This local data is not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly stated otherwise in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the Cora app and its features
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Store and display your tank data, parameters, livestock, and journal entries
  • Generate AI-powered analysis, health reports, and assistant responses through the Cora Assistant
  • Deliver push notifications for maintenance reminders and parameter alerts
  • Communicate with your Cora devices and Neptune Apex controller
  • Improve the app and diagnose technical issues

3. Service Providers

Cora Cloud and the Cora Assistant are operated on secure, industry-leading third-party cloud infrastructure and AI processing providers. These providers act as our sub-processors under data-processing agreements and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations that provide the same or equal protection for your data as described in this policy. They process data only as necessary to deliver Cora features on our behalf.

AI processing provider: OpenAI. The Cora Assistant is powered by OpenAI. When you use the assistant (text chat, voice conversation, health reports or ICP analysis), your question or voice message, together with the tank parameters and readings, livestock list, journal entries and connected-device information needed to answer it, is transmitted to OpenAI, which generates the reply and returns it to us. OpenAI processes this data solely to produce your response. Its handling of data submitted through its API is described in the OpenAI Privacy Policy and the OpenAI enterprise privacy commitments. No data is sent to OpenAI until you have accepted the in-app data-sharing disclosure described in Section 4; if you decline, the assistant is not used and nothing is transmitted.

In addition, the following optional sign-in methods are provided directly to you, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In: optional authentication method
  • Sign in with Apple: optional authentication method

4. Data Consent

We ask for your permission before any of your data is sent for AI processing. The first time you use the Cora Assistant, the app presents an in-app disclosure that states what data will be sent and who it is sent to, and requires you to tap “Agree & Continue” before anything is transmitted. If you decline, nothing is sent.

You can turn consent off at any time from the data consent toggle in Settings. While consent is on, the Cora Assistant may use your saved tank history, parameter data, livestock, journal entries and connected-device information to provide personalized guidance. While it is off, no assistant request is sent and none of your data is transmitted for AI processing.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared with third parties only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Data is processed by our sub-processors, as described in Section 3, solely to provide app functionality
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Your direction: When you explicitly choose to export or share data (for example, configuration files)

6. Data Retention

  • Account data and cloud content are retained as long as your account exists
  • Voice audio is ephemeral and not retained after the session ends
  • ICP report PDFs are retained in Cora Cloud until you delete them or your account
  • Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Notification tokens are retained as long as your account is active

6.1 Anonymized Aquarium Data

When you delete your account, a copy of your aquarium's non-identifying data (tank type, tank volume, water parameter history, lighting schedule, and general livestock categories) may be retained in fully anonymized form to support reef-keeping research and improve Cora's guidance. This anonymized data contains no name, email address, account identifier, device identifier, free-text notes, or photos, and cannot be linked back to you or your account.

You can turn this off at any time before deleting your account in the app under Settings > Cora Assistant > "Contribute anonymized tank data". With the switch off, your aquarium data is permanently deleted together with everything else.

7. Account Deletion and Your Rights

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Settings > Delete Account). When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication account is permanently deleted
  • All data in Cora Cloud associated with your account (tank profiles, parameters, livestock, journal entries, assistant history, health reports, ICP reports, maintenance tasks, alerts) is permanently deleted via a GDPR-compliant cascade deletion
  • All files associated with your account (ICP report PDFs) are permanently deleted
  • Device telemetry data associated with your account is permanently deleted
  • Any stored third-party service sign-in (such as an AquaWiz access token, and a password if you saved one) is permanently deleted
  • If you have left "Contribute anonymized tank data" enabled, a fully anonymized copy of your aquarium's parameter history is retained as described in Section 6.1; it contains nothing that identifies you
  • Local data on your device remains until you uninstall the app

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR), you may also have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your personal data
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Cora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Permissions

Cora may request the following device permissions:

  • Local network access: To discover and communicate with Cora devices and Apex controllers on your Wi-Fi network
  • Bluetooth: To discover and provision new Cora devices during setup
  • Microphone: To capture voice audio for the Cora Assistant voice conversation feature
  • Notifications: To deliver device alerts, parameter alerts, and maintenance reminders
  • Location (Android only): Required by Android for Bluetooth scanning; Cora does not track or store your location

You can deny any permission, but related features may not work correctly.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted local storage for sensitive credentials
  • HTTPS/TLS for all cloud communications
  • Access rules that restrict data access to authenticated users
  • Abuse-prevention safeguards to protect against unauthorized API access

The security of your local network, device, and account credentials is your responsibility.
