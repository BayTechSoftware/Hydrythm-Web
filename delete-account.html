Delete Your Account

Permanent removal of your account and all associated data

Back to home

You can delete your Cora account and all associated data directly from within the app. No email or support request is needed.

How to Delete Your Account

  • Open Cora Mobile on your phone
  • Go to the Settings tab (bottom navigation)
  • Open Account & Subscription and tap Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted
This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Authentication account — your Cora Cloud login is permanently deleted
  • Tank profiles — all tank names, settings, and configurations
  • Water parameters — all stored readings (manual, Apex, device)
  • Livestock records — coral, fish, and invertebrate entries
  • Journal entries — all text and voice journal entries
  • AI Assistant history — all chat and voice conversation transcripts
  • Health reports — all AI-generated health assessments
  • ICP reports — uploaded PDFs and analysis results
  • Maintenance tasks — schedules and completion history
  • Parameter alerts — all configured alert rules
  • Device registrations — device links to your account
  • Device telemetry — all stored sensor readings
  • Push notification tokens — notification registration

What Is NOT Deleted

  • Local app data — preferences stored on your device remain until you uninstall the app
  • Device firmware — your Cora hardware devices continue to operate independently

Data Retention

No user data is retained after account deletion. The deletion is carried out via a GDPR-compliant cascade that permanently removes all associated data from Cora Cloud — authentication, databases, and file storage.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the app to delete your account, contact us at cora@coraiq.tech with the email address associated with your account and we will process the deletion manually.
